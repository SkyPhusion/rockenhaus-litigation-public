This site is the canonical public archive of filed court documents in litigation between Conrad Alan Rockenhaus and Adrienne Rockenhaus (also known as Adrienne Blair and Adrienne Hein, @adezero on social media), across Wayne County 26-104594-DO and 26-102221-PP and Washtenaw 26-737-DO.

Next hearing: August 26, 2026 at 11:30 a.m., in person at the Coleman A. Young Municipal Center (CAYMC), 2 Woodward Avenue, Detroit, MI 48226, Hon. Nicole N. Goodson, Courtroom Room 1501. Eleven motions including the GAL capacity motion. Operative notices: Filing 45 (in-person NOH) and Filing 46 (Proof of Service) (GAL ePraecipe accepted for 8/26).
