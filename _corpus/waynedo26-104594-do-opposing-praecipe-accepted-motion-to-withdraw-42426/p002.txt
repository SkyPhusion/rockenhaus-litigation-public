ELECTRONIC HEARING INFORMATION

Dear Litigant/Counsel of Record,

As of May 11, 2020, the Family Division Court will conduct all court proceedings remotely by Zoom video and/or audio
conferences in an effort to minimize contact with the risk of COVID-19. You are receiving this communication due to
your upcoming hearing as stated on the previous page.

PROCEDURE:
1. Join the Zoom Hearing. All parties should do the following to join your hearing at the time of the call:
 - Go to www.3rdcc.org/zoom or you can find the link under ³Essential Links´
 - Click on ³Family Division- Domestic´
 - Click on ³Join Meeting´next to your Assigned Judge¶s Courtroom.

A ZOOM ACCOUNT IS NOT REQUIRED TO ENTER THE MEETING, BUT IS RECOMMENDED.

IF YOU ARE UNABLE TO CONNECT TO THE MEETING OR REQUIRE AN INTERPRETER, PLEASE CONTACT
THE COURTROOM DIRECTLY. THE COURTROOM CONTACT INFORMATION CAN BE FOUND ON THE
COURT¶S
WEBSITE, www.3rdcc.org/divisions/family-domestic#/list.

2. Enter your name and case number for ³Your Name´For example, ³John Doe 20-123456-DO´

3. Waiting Room. You will be immediately placed into the Assigned Judge¶s waiting room. Upon calling, your
line will be MUTED. Do not hang up or leave the meeting.

4. A member of the court staff will bring you ³into´the virtual hearing at or around the designated time. Before
speaking, please wait until your line is unmuted by the Court.

5. As this is a Court proceeding, professional decorum is expected on ALL Zoom Hearings including, but not limited
to, appropriate dress, background view, and NO eating, drinking, or gum chewing during the proceeding. Please
limit all background noise, distractions, and third party interactions. No minor children are to hear or oversee
your Zoom Hearing at any time. There is to be no recording of Zoom Hearings.

6. Once you are done with the virtual hearing, click ³Leave Meeting´

Please be advised that all Domestic filings including Personal Protection Orders are now electronic. You can visit the
Court¶s website at http://www.3rdcc.org/divisions/family-domestic#/list for detailed instructions under the following
links: ³Family Domestic/FOC Filing Instructions´and ³Personal Protection Order Filing Instructions´
