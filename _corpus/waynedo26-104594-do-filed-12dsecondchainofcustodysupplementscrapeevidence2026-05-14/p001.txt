                                              I. PURPOSE
1. This Second Chain of Custody Supplement is filed in further support of Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's
   Emergency Motion for Preservation Order and Order Prohibiting Spoliation of Evidence, filed on May 13,
   2026, and is a continuation of the Chain of Custody Supplement (the "First Supplement") filed
   contemporaneously with that Motion. By Order entered May 14, 2026, this Honorable Court denied the
   emergency framing of that Motion while leaving the Motion alive on the regular docket for hearing on
   ePraecipe. This Second Supplement therefore supports both the preserved evidentiary record and the
   pending Motion as it proceeds on the regular docket.
2. This Second Supplement adds to the chain-of-custody record three additional public-record Twitter
   scrape datasets captured on May 14, 2026 covering the period from May 12, 2026 forward (collectively,
   the "May 14, 2026 Scrape Evidence"). It further reports two observations that follow from comparison of
   the May 14, 2026 Scrape Evidence with the May 12, 2026 Scrape Evidence already on the record: (a)
   the operational silence of the monitored X-network accounts since May 12, 2026, and (b) a cross-
   platform temporal synchronization on May 9, 2026 between an electronic mail message sent to witness
   Ann Helgren and an X-platform reply by the account @spuntpatrol.

                                    II. CAPTURE METHODOLOGY
3. The May 14, 2026 Scrape Evidence datasets were captured on May 14, 2026 between approximately
   22:02 UTC and 22:03 UTC by Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff Conrad Rockenhaus using the Apify platform's
   apidojo/tweet-scraper actor (https://apify.com), the same actor used for the May 12, 2026 Scrape
   Evidence captures.
4. Three separate queries were submitted to the actor. The first query was a multi-handle date-bounded
   query covering the twelve X account handles preserved in the May 12, 2026 Scrape Evidence (adezero,
   spuntpatrol, LeighCheri_, phatadvert, AllysonClermont, 4chantress, alg0queen, LuceMargarita,
   blackjakkkk, PunkyHomegirl, picklepickleg, p1ratech) with date parameters start: 2026-05-12 and end:
   2026-05-15, configured with maxItems: 1000, sort order Latest, and language filter English. The second
   query targeted the conversation tree of the May 8, 2026 "Dead Man's Switch" broadcast tweet
   (conversation identifier 2052837253408280726). The third query was an open mentions search targeting
   replies to @spuntpatrol or @adezero since May 12, 2026.
