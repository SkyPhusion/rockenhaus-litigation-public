                              EXHIBIT 15
               Affidavit of Ann Helgren, Notarized April 24, 2026

Description:
Notarized affidavit of Ann Helgren (“Annie”), executed under penalty of perjury and notarized April 24,
2026. Helgren is identified in the Redford Township Police Case Report (Exhibit 8) as “friends with
both” parties. Helgren was personally present during the events of March 2, 2026, the day of
Respondent's release from federal custody.

Relevance:
Sworn third-party eyewitness testimony to the events of March 2, 2026. Helgren testifies that: (a)
Petitioner instructed Helgren to make false statements to the Veterans Administration regarding
Respondent's mental health, specifically that Respondent was suicidal, when Helgren had no factual
basis for that statement and directly observed that Respondent was not suicidal; (b) the only service of
the PPO on Respondent was the responding officer reading conditions from Petitioner's cell phone
screen, with no physical copy of the PPO given to Respondent; (c) Petitioner made the demonstrably
false statement to Redford police that Respondent had never resided at 26695 Ross Drive, and
Helgren refused to corroborate that false statement; (d) Petitioner ended the long-standing friendship
with Helgren the next day, March 3, 2026, after Helgren refused to corroborate the false statements.

                    Submitted by Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, Respondent (pro se)
              In Support of Respondent's Motion to Terminate Personal Protection Order
