6. INDEPENDENT POLICE ASSESSMENT THAT CONRAD WAS NOT SUICIDAL:

While at the scene, the Redford Township Police officers independently assessed Conrad.

They determined that he was not suicidal and declined to transport him to any hospital. This

assessment was consistent with my own observation of Conrad that day.

7. PETITIONER'S FALSE STATEMENT TO REDFORD POLICE REGARDING
RESIDENCY: While the Redford Township Police were present on March 2, 2026, and in my

presence, Petitioner stated to the officers that Conrad had never resided at 26695 Ross Drive.

Based on my personal knowledge, this statement by Petitioner was false . I knew Conrad had

resided at 26695 Ross Drive. I refused to support Petitioner's false statement to the police and

did not repeat it to them.

8. PETITIONER CONTINUED TO INSIST CONRAD WAS SUICIDAL AFTER

POLICE ASSESSMENT: Even after the Redford Township Police officers had
independently assessed Conrad and determined he was not suicidal, Petitioner continued to

insist to me that I should tell the Veterans Administration that Conrad was suicidal. I continued

to refuse.

9. I TRANSPORTED CONRAD IN MY PERSONAL VEHICLE: After the Redford

Township Police declined to take any action against Conrad, and because Conrad needed

somewhere to go, I offered to drive him in my personal truck. I drove Conrad to the Veterans
Administration facility.

10. PETITIONER'S INSTRUCTION TO ABANDON CONRAD AT THE VA: While I

was transporting Conrad, Petitioner communicated with me and instructed me to leave Conrad

at the Veterans Administration and abandon him there. I refused. When I refused to comply,

Petitioner threatened to end our friendship.
