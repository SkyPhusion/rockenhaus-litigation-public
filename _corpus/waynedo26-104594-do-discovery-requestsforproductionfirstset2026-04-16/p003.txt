REQUEST NO. 23: All records, correspondence, requests, authorizations, responses, or other documents
related to any attempt by you to obtain Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's medical records, mental health records,

staff on your behalf or on Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's behalf regarding his medical condition, care level, or
treatment.
