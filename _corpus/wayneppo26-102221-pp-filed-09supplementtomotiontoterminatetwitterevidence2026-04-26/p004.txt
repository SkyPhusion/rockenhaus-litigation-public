            F. The Solana Memecoin Blockchain Record (Counterclaim ¶31 Corroboration)
Beginning September 22, 2025, an unidentified third party publicly created a Solana memecoin under
Respondent's name (contract address 8Edpgmsy4kk3vzZ26nRBQJot7jVkrZjAMXHwJzozBAGS). Public X
posts on the day of token creation publicly stated that "100% of the fees [were] going to his wife @adezero."
The complete public Solana blockchain record establishes that across the token's life, 949 transactions
moved $127,986.65 in cumulative USD value, with $63,002.09 of that value flowing to the deployer
wallet FhVo3mqL8PW5pH5U2CN4XE33DokiyZnUwuGpH2hmHLuM that third-party posts publicly
designated as routing fees to Petitioner.
This Supplement does not contend at this stage that Petitioner controlled the deployer wallet or received
realized cash from the token. That determination is properly directed to discovery on Counterclaim ¶31. The
blockchain record is offered as documentary evidence that a substantial financial stream existed under
Respondent's name during the alleged-fear period, was publicly designated to Petitioner's benefit, and was
not publicly disavowed by Petitioner on her verified account.
