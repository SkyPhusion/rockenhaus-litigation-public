                       B. The 7,577-Post Volume Across the Alleged-Fear Period
Beginning September 4, 2025, the day Respondent was taken into federal custody, and continuing through
February 19, 2026 (the day before the ex parte PPO issued), Petitioner posted 7,577 times to her verified
@adezero account, an average of approximately 45 posts per day. Posts in this period consistently
identified Respondent by his full legal name and the affective frame "my husband, Conrad Rockenhaus." On
September 17, 2025, Petitioner posted to a public audience that produced 38,325 views: "My husband,
disabled combat veteran Conrad Rockenhaus, has DISAPPEARED from federal custody... His location is
unknown." (Tweet ID 1968299024882032880.)
A petitioner who fears the respondent does not publicly demand that the federal government disclose the
respondent's whereabouts on a verified account averaging 45 posts per day across the alleged-fear period.

    C. Petitioner's October 14, 2025 Federal-Court Credibility Adjudication (Per Petitioner's Own
                                                 Admission)
By Petitioner's own February 14, 2026 verified-account admission:

          "On October 14, 2025, Judge Stephen J. Murphy III dismissed my reports of sexual
          harassment, gunpoint raids, and medical neglect as 'his wife's behavior.'"

Petitioner publicly admits that on October 14, 2025, approximately four months before swearing similar
allegations under oath in the present PPO Petition, a U.S. District Judge presiding over Respondent's
federal proceedings dismissed Petitioner's sexual-harassment reports as a credibility issue with her conduct
rather than the conduct she alleged. The October 14, 2025 adjudication is admissible against Petitioner
under MRE 801(d)(2) on the question of her general credibility as a sworn-statement witness.
