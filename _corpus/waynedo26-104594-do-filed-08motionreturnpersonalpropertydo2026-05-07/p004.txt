                                    V. INVENTORY AND ACCOUNTING
16. Plaintiff is the only party with current knowledge of the items presently located at the marital residence
    and the items removed from the residence since September 4, 2025. Defendant is entitled to that
    information under MCR 3.206(C) (financial and property disclosure in domestic relations matters) and
    under the Court's inherent authority over marital property pending judgment.
17. Defendant accordingly requests that the Court order Plaintiff to produce, within fourteen (14) days of
    entry of the Order:
a. A sworn inventory of all property of Defendant presently located at 26695 Ross Drive, Redford, Michigan;
b. A sworn accounting of all property of Defendant removed from the residence at any time between
September 4, 2025 and the date of the Order, including without limitation the date of removal, the manner of
removal, the current location of each item, and the identity of any third party in possession; and
c. A sworn statement identifying any item of Defendant's property known by Plaintiff to have been disposed
of, sold, given away, destroyed, or otherwise rendered unrecoverable, including without limitation the date of
disposal, the recipient if any, and the consideration received if any.

                           VI. COURT-SUPERVISED RETRIEVAL MECHANISM
18. Defendant has consistently maintained, and continues to maintain, a behavioral protocol of no direct
    contact with Plaintiff. Defendant seeks the relief in this Motion through court-supervised mechanisms that
    are consistent with that protocol and do not require direct contact between the parties.
19. Defendant accordingly requests that the Court establish a court-supervised retrieval mechanism with the
    following features:
a. A specific date and time for retrieval to be set by the Court;
b. Retrieval to be conducted by a third-party process server, court officer, or other neutral agent designated
by the Court;
c. Plaintiff not required to be present at the retrieval, and at the Court's discretion, ordered absent during the
retrieval window for the safety and dignity of both parties;
d. The neutral agent to videotape the retrieval, to itemize the property removed, and to file a sworn return of
retrieval with the Court within seven (7) days of completion; and
e. Any items that cannot be located or that are missing as compared to the inventory ordered at Section V
above to be documented in the return of retrieval and reserved to Defendant for treatment under Section VII
below.

                 VII. RESERVATION OF DAMAGES FOR NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
20. To the extent any item of Defendant's property described in Section III or referenced in the inventory
    ordered at Section V is not returned to Defendant through the retrieval mechanism set forth at Section
    VI, whether because the item has been disposed of, removed beyond recovery, or otherwise rendered
    unrecoverable, Defendant reserves the right to recover damages for the value of the item plus
    consequential damages and to seek any further relief this Court deems appropriate, including without
    limitation an adverse inference at trial under the spoliation framework set forth in Teel v. Meredith, 284
    Mich App 412 (2009).
