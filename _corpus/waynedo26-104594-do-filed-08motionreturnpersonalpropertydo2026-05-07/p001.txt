 DEFENDANT'S MOTION FOR RETURN OF PERSONAL PROPERTY, RESTORATION
   OF ELECTRONIC ACCOUNT ACCESS, INVENTORY AND ACCOUNTING, AND
              COURT-SUPERVISED RETRIEVAL MECHANISM

NOW COMES Defendant, Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, appearing pro se, and pursuant to MCL 552.401,
MCR 3.206(C), MCR 3.310, and the Court's inherent authority to supervise marital and personal property
pending divorce judgment, respectfully moves this Honorable Court for an Order: (i) directing Plaintiff to
return Defendant's personal property, identification documents, records, and tools currently held at 26695
Ross Drive, Redford, Michigan; (ii) restoring Defendant's access to electronic accounts and the federal
identity-verification chain currently under Plaintiff's operational control; (iii) compelling Plaintiff to produce a
sworn inventory of items at the marital residence and an accounting of items removed since September 4,
2025; (iv) establishing a court-supervised retrieval mechanism that does not require direct contact between
the parties; and (v) reserving Defendant's right to damages for items not returnable. In support thereof,
Defendant states as follows:

                                I. RELATIONSHIP TO PENDING MOTIONS
1. Defendant's Omnibus Three-Layer Motion, filed in this matter on April 15, 2026, addresses preservation
   of evidence, financial disclosure, and a domain-related restraint.
2. Defendant's Motion for Temporary Restraining Order Prohibiting Dissipation of USAA Account Receiving
   Diverted Federal Veterans and Social Security Benefits, filed in this matter on May 7, 2026, addresses
   financial property in the form of federally-protected benefits diverted to an account at USAA Federal
   Savings Bank.
3. This Motion addresses a third and distinct category: Defendant's physical personal property at the
   marital residence and his electronic-account access. The relief sought herein is complementary to, and
   not duplicative of, the relief sought in the Omnibus and TRO Motions. Together, the three motions
   address the full perimeter of property issues subject to this Court's authority pending divorce judgment.
4. A parallel motion seeking related relief is presently pending in Wayne County Personal Protection Order
   matter 26-102221-PP. Defendant intends to withdraw the PPO-track motion upon the assignment of a
   hearing date in this matter, in order to consolidate property relief in the divorce-court forum where this
   Court holds plenary authority under MCL 552.401.
