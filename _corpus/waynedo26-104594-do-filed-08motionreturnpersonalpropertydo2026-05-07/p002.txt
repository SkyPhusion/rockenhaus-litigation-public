5. The parties executed a residential lease for 26695 Ross Drive, Redford, Michigan 48239, commencing
    September 1, 2025, through August 31, 2026. Both parties are named lessees on the lease. The lease is
    filed of record as Exhibit A to Defendant's Motion for Change of Venue and is incorporated by reference.
 6. On September 4, 2025, Defendant was taken into federal custody pursuant to a federal arrest warrant.
    Defendant remained incarcerated at FCI Milan until his release on March 2, 2026. From September 4,
    2025 through March 2, 2026, Defendant had no physical access to the marital residence and no
    opportunity to take possession of any personal property located there.
 7. On or about March 2, 2026, the day of Defendant's release, the Personal Protection Order in Case No.
    26-102221-PP was served. Officers of the Redford Township Police Department were present at the
    marital residence during the service. Plaintiff falsely represented to the officers, in their presence and on
    the record of Redford Township Police Case Report 260003379, that Defendant had never resided at
    26695 Ross Drive. The misrepresentation was contradicted by the recorded lease identifying Defendant
    as a co-lessee.
 8. As a direct result of Plaintiff's misrepresentation to law enforcement, Defendant was prevented from
    retrieving any personal property from the marital residence on the date of his release or at any time
    thereafter. Defendant was rendered homeless following service of the PPO and remained without stable
    housing until March 29, 2026, when he obtained housing at 1690 Brookfield Drive, Ann Arbor, Michigan,
    with the assistance of the Department of Veterans Affairs.
 9. From September 4, 2025 to the present date, Plaintiff has had exclusive operational control over the
    marital residence and the personal property located within it. Defendant has not had the opportunity to
    inventory, access, or remove any of his personal property during that period.
10. Beginning on April 21, 2026, and continuing through April 24, 2026, Defendant made written attempts
    through Plaintiff's then-counsel of record at Aldrich Legal Services, PLLC, to coordinate an informal
    mechanism for retrieval of his personal property. The April 21, 2026 communication included a four-part
    proposal for property retrieval. The April 24, 2026 communication established a deadline of May 1, 2026
    for substantive response regarding a reasonable mechanism for retrieval. No substantive response
    addressing a mechanism for property retrieval was received from Plaintiff or her counsel by the May 1,
    2026 deadline or at any time thereafter. The pre-litigation efforts described in this paragraph demonstrate
    Defendant's good-faith attempt to resolve this matter without recourse to court-supervised relief and
    confirm that informal cooperation is not available.
