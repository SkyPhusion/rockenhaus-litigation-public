VIII. AUTHORITY
21. MCL 552.401 confers on this Court plenary authority over marital property and over the disposition of
    property of the parties pending entry of judgment of divorce. The authority extends to ordering return of
    specific items, accounting, and damages for conversion or dissipation.
22. MCR 3.206(C) requires the parties in a domestic relations matter to make full disclosure of property and
    assets. The disclosure obligation is enforceable by the Court through orders compelling production,
    accounting, and inventory.
23. MCR 3.310 authorizes preliminary injunctive relief, including specific-performance-type relief in the form
    of orders compelling return of specific property, where the elements for injunctive relief are satisfied.
24. The Court's inherent authority over the parties and their property pending judgment supplies further
    foundation for the relief requested, including the supervisory authority to direct the manner and
    conditions under which property retrieval is to be effected.
