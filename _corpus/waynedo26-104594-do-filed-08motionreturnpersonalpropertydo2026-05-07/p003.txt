          IV. ELECTRONIC ACCOUNTS AND FEDERAL IDENTITY-VERIFICATION CHAIN
13. Separate from the physical property described above, Plaintiff retains operational control over electronic
    accounts under Defendant's name and over the federal identity-verification chain that depends on those
    accounts. The accounts and access pathways at issue are the following:
a. Defendant's primary personal email account, conrad@rockenhaus.com;
b. Defendant's secondary personal email account, conradrock@gmail.com;
c. Defendant's professional email account, conrad@cannabytes.net;
d. The two-factor authentication recovery chain dependent on the foregoing email accounts, including
without limitation Defendant's Login.gov account, his ID.me account, his VA.gov account, and his CAC.mil
military Common Access Card account.
14. While Plaintiff retains operational control of the email accounts described above, Defendant cannot
    reliably receive two-factor authentication codes necessary to access his Login.gov, ID.me, VA.gov, or
    CAC.mil accounts. The practical consequence is that Plaintiff exercises operational control, by proxy,
    over Defendant's federal identity-verification posture, his veterans benefits portal, his military records
    portal, and any account that uses email-based two-factor authentication for recovery.
