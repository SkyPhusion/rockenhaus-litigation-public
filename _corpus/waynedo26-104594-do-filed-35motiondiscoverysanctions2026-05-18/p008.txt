                           D. The Information Sought Is Central, Not Peripheral
The Interrogatories track the operative allegations of the Counterclaim. Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant cannot
litigate this divorce, oppose Defendant's Counterclaim, or claim a meritorious defense while categorically
refusing to identify the accounts into which she deposited Defendant's federally protected benefits, the dates
and methods by which she accessed his federal accounts, the location of his identification and personal
property, and the content of her communications with the witness through whom she allegedly engineered
his homelessness on release. The relief sought by Defendant in the Counterclaim, including disgorgement
of diverted federal benefits, return of personal property and federal identification, and restoration of digital
account access, cannot be evaluated without responses to these Interrogatories.

                                 E. Requested Relief Under MCR 2.313(A)
The Court should order Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant to serve full and complete Answers to Defendant's First
Set of Interrogatories Nos. 1 through 20, in writing and under oath, within fourteen days of entry of the
Court's order, and to pay reasonable expenses incurred in obtaining the order pursuant to MCR 2.313(A)(5)
(a). Defendant reserves the right to seek leave to compel Answers to Interrogatories Nos. 21 through 37 if
and when his pending Motion for Leave to Serve Excess Interrogatories is granted.
