                                        B. Failure to Respond Is Total
Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant has not served any written response to Defendant's First Set of Requests for
Production of Documents. The twenty-eight-day deadline under MCR 2.310(C)(2) expired on May 14, 2026
by electronic service and on May 18, 2026 by physical mail service with the three-day mail rule applied. No
documents have been produced. No objections have been served. No privilege log has been served. No
protective order has been sought. The same meet-and-confer documented in Section I.7 covered the
Requests for Production explicitly.
