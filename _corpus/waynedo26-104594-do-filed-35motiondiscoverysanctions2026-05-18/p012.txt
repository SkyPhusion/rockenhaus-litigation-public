                                C. The Independent Path to Default Judgment
Default has already been entered on the Counterclaim under MCR 2.603(A) for failure to plead. Defendant's
Motion for Default Judgment under MCR 2.603(B)(3) is pending. The discovery sanctions sought in this
Motion provide an independent and additional ground for the same ultimate relief, predicated not on failure
to plead but on failure to participate in discovery. The two grounds reinforce each other: Plaintiff/Counter-
Defendant's failure to answer the Counterclaim by the deadline produced the pleading default; her selective
and non-responsive engagement with discovery produced the discovery default. Each, standing alone,
supports the same relief; together they form a record on which the Court may enter default judgment with
confidence that the result is not driven by procedural happenstance.

                                   D. Requested Relief Under MCR 2.313(B)
Defendant requests that the Court enter sanctions under MCR 2.313(B)(2), in the following alternative
formulations, in descending order of preference:
Primary relief. Enter default judgment on Defendant's Counterclaim as a discovery sanction under MCR
2.313(B)(2)(c), independent of the pending Motion for Default Judgment under MCR 2.603(B)(3), with the
further finding that the matters set forth in Defendant's First Set of Requests for Admissions Nos. 1 through
53 are deemed admitted under MCR 2.312(B)(3), and that the factual matters within the scope of
Defendant's First Set of Interrogatories Nos. 1 through 20 are deemed established under MCR 2.313(B)(2)
(a).
Alternative relief. If the Court declines to enter default judgment as a discovery sanction at this stage, then
(a) deem the matters in RFAs 1 through 53 admitted under MCR 2.312(B)(3); (b) deem the matters within
the scope of Interrogatories 1 through 20 established under MCR 2.313(B)(2)(a); (c) prohibit
Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant from supporting or opposing the Counterclaim's allegations of unauthorized
access to federally protected accounts, diversion of federal disability benefits, fraudulent statements to law
enforcement, abuse of Power of Attorney, and coordinated pre-release homelessness engineering under
MCR 2.313(B)(2)(b); (d) order Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant to serve full and complete Answers to
Interrogatories 1 through 20 and responses to all 58 Requests for Production within fourteen days of entry of
the Court's order; and (e) award Defendant the reasonable expenses, including filing fees and costs of
preparation, incurred in obtaining the order.
