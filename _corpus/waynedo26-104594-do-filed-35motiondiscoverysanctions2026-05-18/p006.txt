                                 D. The Pattern Is Strategic, Not Investigative
The identical phrasing across fifty-three Requests, the absence of any specific denial or qualification, the
absence of any acknowledgment of any underlying fact that is plainly true, and the application of the
identical formula to Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's own conduct, her own writings, and her own filings,
demonstrate that no inquiry was undertaken. The Answers are litigation choreography, not discovery. As this
Court is aware from the Verified Motion to Set Aside Default of Counterclaim, Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's
counsel itself represents, at Paragraph 16 of that Motion, that "[o]n April 24, 2026, Plaintiff/Counter-
Defendant instructed Aldrich Legal Services not to continue with this case, and that Plaintiff/Counter-
Defendant would not read or respond to emails from Aldrich Legal Services." Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's
own counsel describes a client who would not engage. The fifty-three identical Answers are the product not
of "reasonable inquiry" but of total client non-engagement.
