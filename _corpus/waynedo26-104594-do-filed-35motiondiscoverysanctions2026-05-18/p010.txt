           D. The Document Production Is the Documentary Predicate of the Counterclaim
Every category of Request for Production seeks documents central to one or more allegations of the
Counterclaim. The financial records establish the diversion of federally protected benefits. The VA and SSA
records establish Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's unauthorized access. The communications with Ann Helgren
and law enforcement establish the pre-release homelessness coordination and false statements to police.
The Cannabytes records establish her wrongful exclusion of Defendant from the business. The federal court
declarations establish her contemporaneous representations of devotion that contradict her later allegations
in the PPO Petition. The trust account records establish her independent source of funds (which she did not
disclose on her MCR 3.206(C) Verified Financial Information Form). Defendant cannot prove these claims at
trial without documentary support, and the documents are in Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's exclusive
possession or control.

                                 E. Requested Relief Under MCR 2.313(A)
The Court should order Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant to serve a written response to Defendant's First Set of
Requests for Production within fourteen days of entry of the Court's order, and to produce all responsive
documents (or, where withheld on privilege grounds, a privilege log conforming to MCR 2.302(B)(7)) within
thirty days, and to pay reasonable expenses incurred in obtaining the order pursuant to MCR 2.313(A)(5)(a).

                      V. SANCTIONS UNDER MCR 2.313(B) ARE WARRANTED
                                             A. Legal Standard
MCR 2.313(B)(2) authorizes the Court, upon failure of a party to obey an order to provide or permit
discovery, or upon a discovery failure independently meriting sanctions, to make "such orders in regard to
the failure as are just." The Rule provides a menu including:

          (a) an order that the matters regarding which the order was made or any other designated facts
          may be taken to be established for purposes of the action in accordance with the claim of the
          party obtaining the order;
          (b) an order refusing to allow the disobedient party to support or oppose designated claims or
          defenses, or prohibiting the party from introducing designated matters in evidence;
