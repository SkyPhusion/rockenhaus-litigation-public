                                          B. The Sanctions Calculus
The Michigan Supreme Court has identified the factors that guide a trial court's exercise of discretion in
selecting sanctions under MCR 2.313(B)(2), including (1) whether the violation was wilful or accidental, (2)
the party's history of refusing to comply with discovery requests, (3) the prejudice to the opposing party, (4)
any actual notice to the defaulting party of the consequences of non-compliance, (5) attempts to correct the
violation, (6) compliance with the time limits set by the rules, (7) whether the failure to provide discovery is
part of a broader pattern of contumacious behavior, and (8) the appropriateness of a lesser sanction.
Frankenmuth Mut Ins Co v ACO, Inc, 193 Mich App 389, 397; 484 NW2d 718 (1992); Bass v Combs, 238
Mich App 16, 26; 604 NW2d 727 (1999).
