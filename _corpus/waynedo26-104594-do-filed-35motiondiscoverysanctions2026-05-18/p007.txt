                                F. Requested Relief Under MCR 2.312(B)(3)
The Court should determine, pursuant to MCR 2.312(B)(3), that Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's Answers to
Defendant's First Set of Requests for Admissions Nos. 1 through 53 are insufficient and that the matters set
forth in those Requests are deemed admitted.

  III. PLAINTIFF/COUNTER-DEFENDANT HAS FAILED TO ANSWER THE INTERROGATORIES

                                              A. Legal Standard
MCR 2.309(B)(3) requires that interrogatories be answered separately, fully, in writing, and under oath
within twenty-eight days of service. MCR 2.313(A)(2)(b) authorizes a party seeking discovery to move for an
order compelling answers when a party "fails to answer an interrogatory submitted under MCR 2.309." MCR
2.313(A)(2)(a) requires that the motion include a certification that the movant has in good faith conferred or
attempted to confer with the party failing to respond.

                                        B. Failure to Answer Is Total
Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant has not served Answers to any Interrogatory in Defendant's First Set of
Interrogatories. The twenty-eight-day deadline under MCR 2.309(B)(3) expired on May 14, 2026 by
electronic service and, at the outside, on May 18, 2026 by physical mail service with the three-day mail rule
applied. No extension was sought. No protective order was sought. No partial response of any kind has
been received as of the date of this Motion. The certification of meet-and-confer under MCR 2.313(A)(2)(a)
is satisfied by the May 13, 2026 11:14 UTC written communication described in Section I.7 above.
Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's selective service of RFA Answers six hours after that communication, in the
same email package that omitted Interrogatory Answers and RFP responses, confirms that the meet-and-
confer was received and considered.

                                  C. Subject Matter of the Interrogatories
The First Set of Interrogatories on which Defendant moves to compel covers Nos. 1 through 20. The relief
Defendant seeks in this Motion is without prejudice to Defendant's separately pending Motion for Leave to
Serve Excess Interrogatories addressing the remaining Interrogatories Nos. 21 through 37. The first twenty
Interrogatories address the following core subjects of the Counterclaim:
Interrogatories 1-4 (Financial accounts). Identification of every bank, credit union, PayPal, Venmo, Cash
App, and cryptocurrency wallet account Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant held, controlled, or had access to
between January 1, 2023 and the present; identification of every account into which Defendant's VA
Disability Compensation payments and SSDI benefits were deposited between September 2025 and the
present; and identification of whether and when Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant accessed Defendant's va.gov
account using his id.me credentials.
Interrogatories 5-7 (VA and SSDI benefits). Month-by-month accounting of VA Disability Compensation
belonging to Defendant that Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant received, accessed, controlled, or disbursed
between September 2025 and the present; month-by-month accounting of SSDI benefits belonging to
Defendant that Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant received, controlled, or disbursed in her capacity as SSA-
appointed Representative Payee; and identification of the representations she made to Defendant or to his
counsel regarding the status of his SSDI payments between September 2025 and March 2026.
Interrogatories 8-9 (Personal property and domains). Identification of all personal property belonging to
Defendant currently in Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's possession (including his identification, financial
documents, and Apple electronics), with current location; and identification of every internet domain
previously registered to or used by Defendant (including rockenhaus.com, skyphusion.com, and
greyponyit.com) that Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant possesses, controls, or has access to.
