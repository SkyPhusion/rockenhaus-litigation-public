4. MARCH 26, 2026 PROCESS SERVER ATTEMPT: On or about March 26, 2026, I retained
a private process server to personally serve Petitioner with my Motion to Terminate the
Personal Protection Order at her residence, 26695 Ross Drive, Redford Township, Michigan,
the same address where the underlying PPO had been served on me on March 2, 2026. The
process server reported to me that on attempting service, Petitioner's vehicle was in the
driveway, indicating Petitioner was at home, but the resident who answered the door
represented that the petitioner, Adrienne Rockenhaus, was unknown at that location. Based
on the process server's report, I executed a Motion for Alternate Service on Form JC 46,
dated March 26, 2026, incorporating the process server's narrative in paragraph 3 of that
motion (Exhibit 7). The Motion for Alternate Service was thereafter granted by this Court
(Exhibit 6), authorizing service of my pleadings on Petitioner through specified alternate
methods, including service through designated agents acting on my behalf. Ann Helgren
acted as my designated agent for service of my subsequent pleadings on Petitioner pursuant
to that order.
