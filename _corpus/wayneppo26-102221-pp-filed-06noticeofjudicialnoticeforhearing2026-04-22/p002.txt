II. Legal Basis
Under In re Sumpter Estate, 166 Mich App 48, 57 (1988), and MCL 600.2106, a trial
court may take judicial notice of another court’s authenticated order or judgment as
prima facie evidence of the facts recited therein. Certified PACER copies constitute
authenticated court records for this purpose. Under MRE 201(b)(2), facts ascertainable

from sources whose accuracy cannot reasonably be questioned are appropriate subjects
of judicial notice. The Court may take judicial notice at any stage of a proceeding.
MRE 201(c), (d).
