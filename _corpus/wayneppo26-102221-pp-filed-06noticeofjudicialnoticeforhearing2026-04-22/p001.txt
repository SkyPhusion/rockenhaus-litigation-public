NOTICE OF INTENT TO REQUEST JUDICIAL NOTICE
AT APRIL 29, 2026 HEARING
Respondent Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, appearing pro se, respectfully notifies Petitioner
and this Honorable Court that at the April 29, 2026 hearing on Respondent’s Motion to
Terminate Personal Protection Order and Petitioner’s Motion to Show Cause,
Respondent will request that the Court take judicial notice pursuant to Michigan Rule
of Evidence 201(b)(2) and MCL 600.2106 of the following two adjudicative facts, each
of which can be accurately and readily determined from sources whose accuracy
cannot reasonably be questioned:
1. On March 6, 2026, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Michigan entered an Order in the consolidated civil-rights action formerly
docketed at 2:25-cv-12716-JMB-SJB severing Petitioner’s claims from
Respondent’s. A certified copy of the Order (ECF No. 27) is attached as Exhibit
A.
2. On December 15, 2025, Petitioner filed in the same consolidated proceeding a
61-page motion purporting to act as Respondent’s “Next Friend” under Whitmore
v. Arkansas, 495 U.S. 149 (1990). A certified copy of Petitioner’s Next Friend
motion (ECF No. 10) is attached as Exhibit B.
