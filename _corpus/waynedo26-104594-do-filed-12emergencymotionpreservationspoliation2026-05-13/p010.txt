                                                 D. Public Interest
54. The public interest in the integrity of the judicial process and the preservation of evidence material to a
    pending civil proceeding is well-established and favors the requested order. See Brenner, 226 Mich.
    App. at 161. The public interest in preventing the use of court-channel concealment as a tactical
    advantage by litigants who have permitted default to enter against them weighs heavily in favor of
    preservation.
