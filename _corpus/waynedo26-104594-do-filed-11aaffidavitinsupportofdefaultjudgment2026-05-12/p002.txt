         IV. CONVERSION OF $26,837.87 IN DIVERTED VA DISABILITY COMPENSATION
 8. From September 2025 through February 2026, I was incarcerated and could not personally manage my
    Department of Veterans Affairs ("VA") Disability Compensation deposits. During this period, Counter-
    Defendant Adrienne Marjorie Rockenhaus accessed my va.gov account using my id.me credentials
    without my authorization. She was not appointed as my VA fiduciary at any relevant time and had no
    lawful authority to access my account, change my direct-deposit destination, or receive my VA Disability
    Compensation payments.
 9. Counter-Defendant used her unauthorized access to my va.gov account to redirect my direct-deposit
    instructions, causing my monthly VA Disability Compensation payments to be deposited into accounts
    under her control rather than into accounts authorized by me.
10. The VA Payment History records I have obtained from VA reflect monthly Disability Compensation
    payments ranging from approximately $4,317 to $4,673 during the period September 2025 through
    February 2026, totaling $26,837.87 in payments diverted to accounts under Counter-Defendant's control.
    The VA Payment History reflects, for each diverted payment, the specific date of payment, the specific
    dollar amount, and the specific routing destination. The VA Payment History also reflects a returned
    payment on October 1, 2025 in the amount of $4,673.44, marked "Undeliverable: Bad Address," during a
    period in which Counter-Defendant represented to me by TRULINCS email that "the VA pay has not
    been deposited for this month."
11. A true and correct copy of the VA Payment History I obtained, together with a redacted summary table
    showing the specific date, amount, and routing destination of each diverted payment, is attached as
    Exhibit B to this Affidavit. Personally identifying information has been redacted.
12. Each of the diverted payments is specifically identifiable by date, amount, and routing destination in the
    VA Payment History. Counter-Defendant had no lawful claim to any of those payments at any point. The
    funds are accordingly property of mine that Counter-Defendant tortiously converted by exercising
    dominion over them inconsistent with my rights, in the meaning of Michigan common-law conversion.
    The total measure of damages established by Exhibit B is $26,837.87.
