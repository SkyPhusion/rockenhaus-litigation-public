                                  VI. PREMARITAL INTERNET DOMAINS
15. I have continuously owned the internet domain rockenhaus.com since 1999, more than two decades
    before my marriage to Counter-Defendant on May 20, 2023. I have continuously owned skyphusion.com
    and greyponyit.com since dates predating the marriage as well. WHOIS history records and registrar
    records reflect these registrations, and a WhoisXMLAPI lookup of rockenhaus.com is included in the
    case file as documentary corroboration.
16. None of these domains was acquired during the marriage. Each is my premarital separate property.
17. Since the filing of the Personal Protection Order in February 2026, Counter-Defendant has continued to
    control rockenhaus.com (including its DNS records, hosting, and email), through her administration of the
    Google Workspace tenancy at rockenhaus.com. She has used rockenhaus.com as a public-advocacy
    hub during the same period in which she has represented to law enforcement and to this Court that she
    is in fear of me. She has retained the registrar credentials, DNS settings, and hosting accounts
    notwithstanding the premarital character of the domains.

                               VII. CANNABYTES, LLC, OFFICER STATUS
18. I am a formally designated officer of Cannabytes, LLC, holding the title of Vice President of Systems
    Engineering. I am listed as such in the LLC formation documents on file with the Michigan Department of
    Licensing and Regulatory Affairs and in the company's internal records. During the marriage, I served as
    the systems engineer and technical infrastructure manager for Cannabytes, LLC, building, maintaining,
    and administering the company's technical systems. Counter-Defendant was approved by U.S.
    Probation as my employer through Cannabytes, LLC.
19. Since the filing of the Personal Protection Order, I have been excluded from all Cannabytes, LLC
    operations, systems, and accounts. I have received no accounting of business revenues, expenses,
    assets, or liabilities. I have no access to the company's financial records, customer accounts, or internal
    communications.

            VIII. VERIFIED STATEMENT DEFECTS (MCR 3.206(C), SCAO FORM FOC 23)
20. Counter-Defendant filed a Verified Statement on SCAO Form FOC 23 pursuant to MCR 3.206(C) with
    her Verified Complaint on April 9, 2026. I have reviewed that Verified Statement as filed in the case
    record. It is facially incomplete as to Counter-Defendant's own income. Item 18 (Occupation) is
    completed as "Self Employed." Item 19 (Business/Employer) is completed as "Cannabytes, LLC." Item
    20 (Gross weekly Income) is left blank. By contrast, the same form fields completed by Counter-
    Defendant about me at items 41 and 43 include both my occupation and a specific dollar figure for gross
    weekly income.
21. From my direct testimonial knowledge gained during the marriage, I also know the following facts about
    Counter-Defendant's income that are not disclosed anywhere in the Verified Statement. Counter-
    Defendant is the income beneficiary of a third-party-settled trust established by her deceased mother.
    The trustee is her nephew, Robby. Counter-Defendant receives approximately $2,000 per month in
    distributions from the trust. The trust has been distributing to Counter-Defendant on a recurring monthly
    basis throughout the marriage and through the present. To my knowledge, the corpus is held subject to a
    spendthrift provision; the recurring monthly distributions to Counter-Defendant are post-distribution.
22. None of the trust facts in Paragraph 21 is disclosed in Counter-Defendant's Verified Statement.
    Combined with the blank gross-weekly-income field at item 20, the Verified Statement is incomplete both
    on its face and substantively as to "all sources of income" required by MCR 3.206(C). I bring these facts
    to this Court's attention not to seek separate relief in the Motion that this Affidavit supports, but to inform
    the Court's exercise of discretion in entering default judgment and to provide context for any subsequent
    damages-hearing or property-division proceedings under MCL 552.19, MCL 552.401, and MCL 552.452.
