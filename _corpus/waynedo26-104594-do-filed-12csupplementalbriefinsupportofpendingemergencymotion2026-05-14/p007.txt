                             IV. SIGNIFICANCE TO THE PENDING MOTION
29. The Pending Motion requests preservation directives addressed to X Corp. (covering the named X
    accounts including @rosebuttcheeks), Meta Platforms, Inc. (including Facebook and Threads), and
    Google LLC (including the email account ecigaretteslut@gmail.com), among other targets. The four
    findings developed in §III above strengthen the inferential basis for each component of the relief already
    requested in the Pending Motion.
30. As to the X Corp. preservation directive: The cessation finding combined with the @rosebuttcheeks
    documentary-attribution finding (§III.A) demonstrates that the operator of the coordinated network is
    operationally sophisticated, is aware of the litigation-preservation posture, and has implemented a
    private-identity continuity architecture under cover of which case-narrative activity continues. The
    continued posting from the @rosebuttcheeks account during the same period that the publicly-
    attributable accounts have gone silent is a contemporaneous demonstrated risk that operator-attributable
    X-platform evidence will be deleted, account-disabled, or migrated to additional concealed accounts
    absent immediate preservation. The @BiancaWestfall finding (§III.C) materially expands the scope of
    accounts requiring X Corp. preservation to include an account whose existing posts include original-
    content hostile-act posts targeting Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's federal-court attorney and identifying
    federal correctional officers as "Neo-Nazi."
31. As to the Meta Platforms preservation directive: The operational migration finding (§III.D)
    demonstrates active and continuing witness-targeting activity on Facebook (the "Demi-Jo Matthews"
    account; the "Dead Man's Switch" post; the Michigan Missing Persons Alerts group share) and on
    Threads (the May 14, 2026 morning friend-request contact to Witness Helgren). Each of these is an
    ongoing operational use of a Meta platform during the period between filing of the Pending Motion and
    ruling on the Pending Motion, creating an elevated and continuing risk of spoliation absent preservation.
