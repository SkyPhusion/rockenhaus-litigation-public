                                           II. PURPOSE OF FILING
The foregoing affidavits are filed for the record in this matter. They are particularly relevant to the following
pending matters before this Court:
(a) Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's Motion for Default Judgment on Counterclaim, filed May 12, 2026 under
MCR 2.603(B)(3), as evidentiary substrate for the matters alleged in the original Counterclaim now admitted
by operation of the default entered against Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant on May 12, 2026;
(b) Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's Motion to Determine Sufficiency of Answers to Requests for Admissions, to
Compel Responses to Interrogatories and Requests for Production of Documents, and for Discovery
Sanctions, filed May 18, 2026 (Filing #35), as evidentiary substrate for the matters alleged in
Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's First Set of Requests for Admissions, in particular those Requests addressing
Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's communications with Ms. Helgren, her statements to law enforcement, and her
possession of Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's personal property and identification; and
(c) Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's First Amended Counterclaim, filed May 18, 2026 (Filing #34) with leave
sought concurrently in Filing #33, as evidentiary substrate for Section XIII (Coordinated Multi-Platform
Harassment Operation), in particular the matters pleaded at paragraphs 80 through 91 concerning the
cross-platform Facebook conduct, the May 4, 2026 Dead Man's Switch post, and the May 9, 2026 vacation
of 26695 Ross Drive.

                   III. TRANSPARENCY NOTE REGARDING PARALLEL PPO FILING
Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff also files contemporaneously in the related personal protection order matter
(Wayne County Case No. 26-102221-PP) a Supplemental Witness Affidavit of Ann Helgren Regarding
Events of May 4 through May 6, 2026. The substantive matters covered in that PPO-captioned
Supplemental Affidavit are also covered in paragraphs 17 through 20 of the Comprehensive Witness
Affidavit of Ann Helgren attached hereto as Exhibit A. The parallel PPO filing is made for the PPO docket
record on a PPO-specific frame; no duplication or inconsistency between the two affidavits is intended, and
Ms. Helgren swears to the same operative facts in each.

                                    IV. NO RELIEF SOUGHT BY THIS NOTICE
This Notice does not seek any specific relief and is filed for the record only. Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff
reserves all rights to reference the attached affidavits as evidentiary substrate in any future filing, motion,
hearing, or trial in this matter.
