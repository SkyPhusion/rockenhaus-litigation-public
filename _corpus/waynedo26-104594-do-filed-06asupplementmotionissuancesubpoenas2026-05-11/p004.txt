                                            C. Procedural Authority
25. The procedural rule governing issuance and service of all three supplemental subpoenas is MCR 2.305,
    which authorizes issuance of subpoenas to non-parties for production of documents and tangible things
    and requires fourteen days' pre-service notice to opposing counsel. MCR 2.305(A)(2). Defendant will
    provide Plaintiff's counsel with the requisite fourteen days' notice before service of each supplemental
    subpoena upon issuance.

                           IV. SCOPE OF THE SUPPLEMENTAL SUBPOENAS
                 A. Scope of the Michigan Department of State Subpoena (Eleventh Target)
26. The supplemental subpoena to the Michigan Department of State seeks disclosure of records in the
    custody of the Department of State Record Lookup Unit pertaining to license plate 7RBK87, narrowly
    scoped to:
a. The current vehicle registration record, including registered owner, registered address of record, vehicle
identification number, and registration effective and expiration dates;
b. The current title record, including titled owner, lien holder, and title-issuance date;
c. Ownership-transfer history for the period January 1, 2024 through the date of subpoena service;
d. Plate-issuance history, if the plate has been transferred between vehicles, for the period January 1, 2024
through the date of subpoena service;
e. Business or fleet registration designation, if applicable; and
f. Insurance certification of record filed with the Department, to the extent retained.
27. The scope is limited to what is reasonably necessary to identify the registered owner and ownership
    history of the vehicle whose involvement in the events of May 9, 2026 is at issue. No financial detail (no
    payment-card or bank-account information) is sought.

                          B. Scope of the Google LLC Subpoena (Twelfth Target)
28. The supplemental subpoena to Google LLC seeks disclosure of basic subscriber information in the
    custody of Google LLC pertaining to the Gmail account ecigaretteslut@gmail.com, narrowly scoped to
    the categories expressly authorized by 18 U.S.C. § 2703(c)(2):
a. Subscriber name(s) of record at account creation and as updated thereafter;
b. Subscriber address(es) of record at account creation and as updated thereafter;
c. Account creation date, length of service, and account-status history;
d. Recovery email address(es) and recovery telephone number(s) associated with the account;
e. IP-address logs reflecting account creation and account access for the period April 1, 2026 through the
date of subpoena service, including timestamps and originating IP addresses for sign-in events and email-
send events;
f. Means and source of payment for any paid Google service associated with the account, if applicable,
limited to issuer name, last four digits of card number, and billing name and address; and
g. Identifiers of any other Google account associated with ecigaretteslut@gmail.com by recovery email,
recovery telephone number, or shared device, to the extent such association is reflected in Google's
records.
