                   C. Scope of the Meta Platforms, Inc. Subpoena (Thirteenth Target)
30. The supplemental subpoena to Meta Platforms, Inc. seeks disclosure of basic subscriber information in
    the custody of Meta Platforms, Inc. pertaining to the Facebook account identified by:
    Display name: "Demi-Jo Matthews";
    Profile URL: https://www.facebook.com/amanda.berman.376; and
    Numeric Facebook user-ID (owning_profile_id): 100003851214028.
The records sought are narrowly scoped to the categories expressly authorized by 18 U.S.C. § 2703(c)(2):
a. Subscriber name(s) of record at account creation and as updated thereafter, including any prior account-
name changes recorded by Meta Platforms;
b. Subscriber address(es) of record at account creation and as updated thereafter;
c. Account creation date, length of service, and account-status history;
d. Email address(es) of record associated with the account at any point during account life;
e. Telephone number(s) of record associated with the account at any point during account life;
f. IP-address logs reflecting account creation and account access for the period April 1, 2026 through the
date of subpoena service, including timestamps and originating IP addresses for login events, post-
publication events, and comment-publication events;
g. Means and source of payment for any paid Meta service associated with the account, if applicable, limited
to issuer name, last four digits of card number, and billing name and address; and
h. Identifiers of any other Meta-platform account (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, WhatsApp) associated
with the Demi-Jo Matthews account by shared email, shared telephone number, shared payment
instrument, or shared device, to the extent such association is reflected in Meta's records.

31. The scope expressly excludes the content of any communication, post body, photograph, video,
    message, or other content data. No private-message content, no post-body content, no comment-body
    content, no photograph or video file content, and no other content data is requested.
