EXHIBIT B

 HANDWRITTEN SIGN AT PETITIONER'S RESIDENCE DIRECTED AT MS. HELGREN, MARCH
                                   29, 2026
Description. Photograph of a handwritten sign affixed at the kitchen window of Petitioner's residence at
26695 Ross Drive, Redford, Michigan 48239. The sign is handwritten in blue ink on white paper, taped at
the corners with yellow tape. The salutation reads "Anne Helgen," a variant misspelling of Ann Helgren. The
body text demands that Ms. Helgren cease unspecified "legal abuse" against Petitioner and stop appearing
within Petitioner's sight immediately. A second handwritten sign is partially visible below it, also addressed
to "Anne Helgren," referencing a March 3, 2026 written communication and accusing Ms. Helgren of having
put "threatening documents" on Petitioner's property "on behalf of my rapist." Photograph taken on or about
March 29, 2026.
Relevance. First documented entry in the pattern-of-conduct timeline against Ms. Helgren analyzed at
Section IV of the underlying Motion (Paragraph 28). Establishes the temporal start of the witness-targeting
pattern, six weeks prior to the May 4, 2026 Facebook Post. The sign has no legal force, as Ms. Helgren is
not a party to this PPO matter and is under no court order requiring her to maintain any distance from
Petitioner (Paragraph 32). Together with the May 5, 2026 printed sign (Exhibit C), the May 4, 2026
Facebook Post (Exhibit A), the Demi-Jo Matthews missing-persons-group share (Exhibit E), and the Demi-
Jo Matthews comment on Ms. Helgren's wall (Exhibit F), this exhibit establishes a sustained pattern of
escalating conduct directed at Ms. Helgren whose only role in this matter is as a credible factual witness
adverse to Petitioner's PPO posture (Paragraph 33).
