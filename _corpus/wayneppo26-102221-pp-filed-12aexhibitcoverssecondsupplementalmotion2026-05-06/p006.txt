EXHIBIT E

DEMI-JO MATTHEWS ACCOUNT SHARE OF MAY 4, 2026 FACEBOOK POST INTO "MICHIGAN
       MISSING PERSONS ALERTS" (1,700-MEMBER PUBLIC GROUP), MAY 5, 2026
Description. Screenshot of a post by the Facebook account displayed under the name "Demi-Jo Matthews"
within the Facebook group "Michigan Missing Persons Alerts," a public Facebook group with approximately
1,700 members at the time of the share. The post shares the May 4, 2026 Facebook Post (Exhibit A) and
adds the framing text: "please look into this. He is my friend and I am extremely concerned. He's been
missing for at least a month. His location is pinging in Ann Arbor." The shared content is displayed alongside
an embedded preview of the underlying Post under the byline "Conrad Rockenhaus." Screenshot captured
on or about May 5, 2026.
Relevance. Documents the propagation of the May 4, 2026 Facebook Post (Exhibit A) into a 1,700-member
public missing-persons audience, addressed at Section III.B of the underlying Motion (Paragraphs 16
through 19). The propagation expanded the audience of the Post by at least one order of magnitude beyond
Respondent's personal Facebook network, increasing proportionally the risk to the named persons of
inbound contact, harassment, or other conduct from third parties acting on the false predicate. The "His
location is pinging in Ann Arbor" representation draws on the same AirTag-derived geolocation as the GPS
telemetry block in the original Post, namely a non-public data source pointing uniquely to Petitioner. The
shared geolocation-data source is the load-bearing forensic indicium of authorship analyzed at Paragraph
24.c, supporting the inference that the Demi-Jo Matthews Account is operated by or at the direction of
Petitioner and that the Post itself was authored by Petitioner.
Submitted by Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, Respondent (pro se)
