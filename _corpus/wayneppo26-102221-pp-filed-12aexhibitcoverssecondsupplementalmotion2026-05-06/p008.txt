EXHIBIT G

                                     AFFIDAVIT OF ANN HELGREN
Description. Sworn affidavit of Ann Helgren regarding the events of May 4 through May 6, 2026 as
observed by her personally, including (a) her direct observation of the May 4, 2026 Facebook Post (Exhibit
A) on her personal Facebook feed and her telephone notification to Respondent thereafter; (b) her in-person
walk-in to Redford Police Department on May 5, 2026 with Ms. Jordan and Respondent and the resulting
Case 26-7649 (Exhibit D); (c) her observation of the May 5, 2026 printed sign at Petitioner's residence
(Exhibit C); and (d) her relationship to the Demi-Jo Matthews Account, including her inability to view the
Account or its activity (the configuration analyzed at Paragraph 23 of the Motion). Notarized.
Relevance. Witness corroboration for the events described at Sections II, III, IV, and V of the underlying
Motion. Anchors the foundational testimony at Paragraphs 7 (Ms. Helgren's telephone notification to
Respondent regarding the Post), 23 (Ms. Helgren's inability to view the Demi-Jo Matthews Account), and 34
through 37 (the May 5 walk-in to Redford Police Department and resulting welfare check). Provides
foundation under MRE 901 for the screenshots at Exhibits A, E, and F to the extent Ms. Helgren personally
observed the underlying Facebook content. Ms. Helgren's prior affidavit, filed as Exhibit 15 to the April 25,
2026 evidence bundle, is incorporated by reference and is not displaced by this affidavit; this affidavit
supplements her prior testimony with additional matters arising from the events of May 4 through May 6,
2026.
