EXHIBIT H

                                  AFFIDAVIT OF PAMELA JORDAN
Description. Sworn affidavit of Pamela Jordan regarding the events of May 4 through May 5, 2026 as
observed by her personally, including (a) her observation of conditions at Petitioner's residence at 26695
Ross Drive, Redford, Michigan 48239 in the days surrounding May 4, 2026, including the absence of
expected routine activity; and (b) her in-person walk-in to Redford Police Department on May 5, 2026 with
Ms. Helgren and Respondent and the resulting welfare check at Petitioner's residence under Case 26-7649.
Notarized.
Relevance. Witness corroboration for the events described at Sections IV and V of the underlying Motion.
Anchors the foundational testimony at Paragraphs 34 through 37, namely the May 5, 2026 walk-in to
Redford Police Department, the matters reported to Redford Police Department, and the welfare check
conducted at Petitioner's residence. Ms. Jordan was identified in the May 4, 2026 Facebook Post (Exhibit A)
as one of two persons "responsible" for Respondent's purported death, and her affidavit anchors the
inversion-of-narrative observation at Paragraph 38: namely that the persons named in the Post as
responsible for Respondent's death are the same persons who, on the day after the Post, asked Redford
Police Department to make sure Petitioner was physically safe.

Submitted by Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, Respondent (pro se)
In Support of Respondent's Second Supplemental Motion in Support of Termination of Personal Protection
