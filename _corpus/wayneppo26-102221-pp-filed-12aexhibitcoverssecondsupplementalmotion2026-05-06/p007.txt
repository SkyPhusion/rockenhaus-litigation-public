EXHIBIT F

DEMI-JO MATTHEWS ACCOUNT COMMENT ACCUSING MS. HELGREN OF HOMICIDE, MAY 6,
                                 2026
Description. Screenshot of a comment by the Facebook account displayed under the name "Demi-Jo
Matthews" on a photograph posted by Ann Helgren on her personal Facebook account on April 24, 2026.
The April 24, 2026 photograph depicts Respondent and Ms. Helgren together at Ms. Helgren's residence,
both alive, both in good spirits, more than ten days before the May 4, 2026 Facebook Post (Exhibit A). The
Demi-Jo Matthews comment, posted on or about May 6, 2026, reads in pertinent part: "Is that right before
you killed him?" followed by a Facebook share URL. Screenshot captured on or about May 6, 2026.
Relevance. Documents direct witness harassment by the Demi-Jo Matthews Account against Ms. Helgren,
addressed at Section III.C of the underlying Motion (Paragraphs 20 through 23) and Section IV.C
(Paragraph 31). The comment publicly accuses a witness in this PPO matter of homicide, on the witness's
own publicly-visible Facebook wall, on the eve of the merits hearing on Respondent's Motion to Terminate.
On information and belief, the Demi-Jo Matthews Account has Ms. Helgren blocked, with the result that Ms.
Helgren cannot view the comment in real time but Ms. Helgren's social network can. That configuration is
consistent with intent to damage Ms. Helgren's reputation among persons in her social network while
preventing Ms. Helgren from observing or addressing the accusation directly, and is analyzed at Paragraph
24.f of the Motion as a discrete indicium that the Account is part of a coordinated effort directed at the
witness rather than ordinary social-media activity by an unaffiliated user.

Submitted by Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, Respondent (pro se)
