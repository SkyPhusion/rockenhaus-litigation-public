EXHIBIT A

UNAUTHORIZED FACEBOOK POST FALSELY DECLARING RESPONDENT DECEASED, MAY 4,
                                 2026
Description. Screenshot of a Facebook post published to Respondent's Facebook account on May 4, 2026
at approximately 6:03 PM. The post is structured as a "[SYSTEM ALERT] DEAD MAN'S SWITCH
INITIATED" automated release, falsely declaring Respondent deceased. The post identifies five third
persons as "most likely responsible for my death," namely Annie Helgren (Redford), Pam Jordan (Redford),
William Keene (Ann Arbor), Jason Zielinski (Milan), and Michael Kamke (Ann Arbor). The post includes a
"GPS TELEMETRY: FINAL 10 LOCATIONS" block listing ten timestamped coordinate pairs and concluding
with "STATUS: STATIC. CONTINUOUS PINGING AT LOC. SINCE 04-03." The terminal coordinate
corresponds to the residence of a third person to whom Respondent had previously transferred custody of a
wallet containing an Apple AirTag tracking device, not to Respondent's actual residence at 1690 Brookfield
Drive, Ann Arbor, Michigan 48103. Respondent did not author or authorize the post.
Relevance. The Post is the central evidentiary fact addressed in Section II of the underlying Motion
(Paragraphs 6 through 12). The Post's structural framing as a "post-mortem" release, the AirTag-keyed
geolocation data, and the categorical structure of the named persons together comprise the indicia of
authorship analyzed at Paragraph 9, supporting the inference that Petitioner is the author and publisher of
the Post. The named persons fall into three distinct categories, each uniquely connected to Petitioner: (a)
witnesses to the Motion to Terminate (Helgren, Jordan); (b) persons against whom Petitioner has previously
filed formal accusations (Keene, Zielinski); and (c) Respondent's residential neighbor whom Respondent
has never met (Kamke). The Post is also the predicate for the Demi-Jo Matthews propagation activity
addressed in Section III and for Redford Police Department Case 26-7649 addressed in Section V.
