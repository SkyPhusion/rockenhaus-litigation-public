EXHIBIT C

       PRINTED SIGN AT PETITIONER'S RESIDENCE FALSELY ASSERTING "NO-CONTACT
                     DIRECTIVE" AGAINST MS. HELGREN, MAY 5, 2026
Description. Photograph of a typewritten and printed sign affixed at Petitioner's residence at 26695 Ross
Drive, Redford, Michigan 48239, materially distinct from the handwritten March 29, 2026 sign (Exhibit B) in
form and content. The text reads, in pertinent part: "Annie Helgren has a no-contact directive and is not
allowed on my property. If Anne Helgren steps on my property she will be reported for trespassing."
Photograph taken on or about May 5, 2026.
Relevance. Second documented entry in the pattern-of-conduct timeline against Ms. Helgren analyzed at
Section IV.B of the underlying Motion (Paragraphs 29 through 30). The sign affirmatively misrepresents Ms.
Helgren's legal status by asserting the existence of a "no-contact directive" that has not been entered by this
Court or by any other court of competent jurisdiction. The sign also threatens criminal complaint for
trespassing against Ms. Helgren, positioning Petitioner to make a police report misrepresenting Ms. Helgren
as the subject of a court order, which, if relied upon by responding officers, could expose Ms. Helgren to
wrongful detention or arrest. The sign was posted on or about the same day Ms. Helgren and Ms. Jordan
reported the May 4, 2026 Facebook Post (Exhibit A) to Redford Police Department, the same day a welfare
check was conducted at Petitioner's residence under Redford Police Department Case 26-7649 (Exhibit D).
Submitted by Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, Respondent (pro se)
In Support of Respondent's Second Supplemental Motion in Support of Termination of Personal Protection
