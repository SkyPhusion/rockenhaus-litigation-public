                              Ill. GROUNDS FOR DIVORCE

 8. There has been a breakdown of the marriage relationship to the extent that the

    objects of matrimony have been destroyed and there remains no reasonable

    likelihood that the marriage can be preserved, within the meaning of MCL 552.6.

 9. Plaintiff brings this action solely to protect his legal rights and to obtain a lawful

    dissolution of the marriage. Plaintiff does not bring this action out of hostility, but

    recognizes that the parties' circumstances have made continuation of the marriage

    relationship untenable.

10. Plaintiff affirmatively requests that the Court order the parties to participate in

    counseling and/or mediation prior to or during the pendency of this proceeding, in

    order to facilitate a fair, amicable, and just resolution of all matters incident to this

    divorce. Plaintiff believes that professional guidance may assist in resolving

    property and financial disputes without unnecessary litigation.
