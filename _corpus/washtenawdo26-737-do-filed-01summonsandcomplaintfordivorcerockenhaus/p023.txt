26. Further evidence of Defendant's bad faith in obtaining and maintaining the PPO is

    provided by her own conduct following its issuance. In or about October 2025, while

    Plaintiff was in federal custody at FCI Milan , he sent Defendant a personal

    photograph of himself along with a handwritten note expressing love and affection ,

    addressed to her at the marital residence. The PPO did not exist at that time ,

    having not been filed until February 20, 2026. Rather than discard or retain the

    envelope, Defendant took the affirmative step of locating Plaintiff's current address

    and forwarding the envelope to him at the Delonis Center homeless shelter, 312

    West Huron Street, Ann Arbor, Michigan . Plaintiff received this forwarded envelope

    on or about April 4, 2026. The envelope bears Plaintiff's inmate ID and the FCI

    Milan return address, with a forwarding label affixed by Defendant directing delivery

    to Plaintiff's shelter address. This conduct, actively seeking out Plaintiff's location

    and initiating mail contact with him while simultaneously maintaining a PPO that

    prohibits him from contacting her, is wholly inconsistent with any genuine fear of

    Plaintiff and constitutes further evidence that the PPO was obtained and

    maintained for retaliatory and financial purposes rather than for genuine protection.

    This evidence has been submitted as a supplemental filing in the PPO proceeding

    (Wayne County Case No. 26-102221-PP, filed April 6, 2026).
