20. Prior to and during the period of financial misconduct described herein, Defendant

    held a Power of Attorney granted to her by Plaintiff. Plaintiff revoked this Power of

    Attorney on January 25, 2026. On February 17, 2026, Plaintiff served Defendant

    with a certified written revocation of her authority to receive, manage, or direct any

    of his funds. Despite this revocation, Defendant continued to intercept and retain

    Plaintiff's VA Disability Compensation payment for March 2026. Significantly, the

    PPO against Plaintiff was filed on February 20, 2026, only three days after the

    certified revocation of her financial authority was served, further establishing that

    the PPO was filed in direct retaliation for the loss of her access to Plaintiff's funds

    rather than out of any genuine fear. Furthermore, in the PPO petition itself,

    Defendant    falsely   characterized     Plaintiff's   premarital   domains,    including

    rockenhaus.com, as her own business property, and sought court orders

    prohibiting Plaintiff from accessing his own digital infrastructure. This false

    characterization in a sworn court filing , combined with the retaliatory timing of the

    PPO filing , further evidences Defendant's pattern of using legal mechanisms to

    maintain financial control over Plaintiff's assets and to retaliate against his efforts to

    protect himself.
