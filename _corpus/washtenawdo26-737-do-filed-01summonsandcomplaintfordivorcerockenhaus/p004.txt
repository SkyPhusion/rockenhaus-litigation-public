2. Plaintiff is a United States Navy veteran, having first enlisted in December 1999

   received an Honorable Discharge, as reflected in his DD Form 214. Plaintiff is rated

   veteran , and his VA Disability Compensation constitutes his sole source of monthly

   income. This context is directly relevant to the financial misconduct alleged herein,

   as Defendant, while acting as Plaintiff's Representative Payee and holding his

   Power of Attorney, diverted and withheld the federally protected disability benefits

   of a 100% disabled combat veteran.
