25. On March 4 and 5, 2026, Annie made multiple attempts on Plaintiff's behalf to

    retrieve Plaintiff's personal property and secure funds for Plaintiff from Defendant.

    Defendant refused both requests on each occasion. In justifying her refusal to

    return Plaintiff's property or release any funds to him, Defendant told Annie that

    doing so would be "funding my rapist." This statement is significant for several

    reasons . First, it demonstrates that Defendant was using the rape allegation, which

    emerged on February 12, 2026, the same day Plaintiff filed protective legal

    motions, not as a genuine safety concern but as a financial justification to continue

    withholding Plaintiff's property and funds. Second, it establishes that Defendant's

    continued denial of Plaintiff's property and money was deliberate, ongoing, and

    unambiguous. Third, it further corroborates that Annie served as a direct witness to

    Defendant's conduct across multiple incidents, including the false statements to

    police on March 2, the false VA commitment attempt, and now the post-eviction

    property and financial denial on March 4 and 5.
