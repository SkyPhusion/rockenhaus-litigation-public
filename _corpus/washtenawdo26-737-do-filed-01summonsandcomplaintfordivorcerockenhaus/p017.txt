(j) Full accounting of, and equitable relief regarding, Plaintiff's technical contributions

   to Cannabytes, LLC, a digital software and web services business. Defendant

   established the business relationships with the business's clients, including

   Humboldt CSI and Radogear.com, the latter of which the parties mutually

   concluded the business relationship with . Plaintiff served as the primary technical

   operator and systems maintainer of the business prior to his arrest in September

   2025, providing the technical labor and infrastructure maintenance upon which the

   business's operations and client services depended. Defendant has since assumed

   exclusive control of the business, its digital infrastructure, domains, servers, and

   revenue, while Plaintiff has been locked out of all associated accounts and

   business assets . Furthermore, Plaintiff has reason to believe that Defendant

   introduced a third party, Sibin Grasic, as a partner in Cannabytes, LLC without

   Plaintiff's knowledge, consent, or proper involvement, notwithstanding Plaintiff's

   ownership interest in the business. If true, the introduction of a new partner into a

   business in which Plaintiff holds an ownership stake, without his authorization, may

   constitute a breach of fiduciary duty and could materially affect the value of

   Plaintiff's interest. Plaintiff requests that the Court order a full accounting of all

   Cannabytes, LLC revenue, expenses, assets, and any changes to the business's

   ownership or partnership structure from the inception of the business through the

   present, including any agreements entered into with Sibin Grasic or any other third

   party, and that the Court make an equitable determination of any compensation

   owed to Plaintiff for the value of his technical contributions and ownership interest

   in the business during the marriage.
