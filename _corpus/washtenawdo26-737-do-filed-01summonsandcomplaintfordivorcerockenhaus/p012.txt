18. The financial misconduct described in this Section occurred during a period when

    Plaintiff was particularly vulnerable and wholly unable to monitor or protect his

    financial interests. From September 2025 through March 2, 2026, Plaintiff was in

    federal custody at FCI Milan, Michigan, including an extended period in the Special

    Housing Unit (SHU), a form of solitary confinement. During this time, Plaintiff

    suffered from a documented Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) and a seizure disorder,

    was medically isolated, had severely limited communication access, and was

    entirely dependent on Defendant as his Representative Payee and Power of

    exploited this period of Plaintiff's maximum vulnerability to divert his federal

    benefits, file her taxes separately, and execute the premeditated plan described in

    Section IV, knowing that Plaintiff had no means to discover or prevent what she

    was doing.
