13. Evidence of Defendant's premeditated financial separation from Plaintiff predates

    the PPO and the February 2026 escalation by several months. In or about October

    2025, while simultaneously acting as Plaintiff's Representative Payee and

    collecting his VA Disability Compensation and SSDI benefits, Defendant filed her

    federal income taxes for both the 2023 and 2024 tax years using the status of

    'married filing separately.' This filing status, chosen while Defendant was actively

    receiving Plaintiff's federal disability benefits as his designated Representative

    Payee and purported Next Friend , reflects a deliberate financial and legal

    distancing from Plaintiff on paper while continuing to access his income. Plaintiff

    has TRULINCS communications documenting Defendant's tax filing decisions. The

    fact that Defendant was quietly and systematically separating her financial and tax

    obligations from Plaintiff as early as October 2025, months before she claimed to

    be in sudden fear for her safety, is further evidence that the PPO and the

    subsequent campaign of financial misconduct were part of a premeditated plan

    rather than a spontaneous response to any threat posed by Plaintiff.
