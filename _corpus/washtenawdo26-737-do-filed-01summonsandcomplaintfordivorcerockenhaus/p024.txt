27. In a further effort to maintain fraudulent control over Plaintiff's VA Disability

    Compensation and SSDI benefits, Defendant engaged in a coordinated campaign

    to have Plaintiff falsely committed to a VA psychiatric facility, both prior to and on

    the day of his release from federal custody . Defendant made false reports to VA

    medical staff claiming that Plaintiff was suicidal. In support of these false reports,

    Defendant fabricated a claim that Plaintiff's own mother was telling Defendant that

    Plaintiff was sending her text messages expressing suicidal ideations, a claim that

    was knowingly and demonstrably false, as Plaintiff was incarcerated and did not

    have access to a phone or any means of sending such messages. Defendant then

    solicited the same mutual acquaintance, Annie, to appear at the VA and falsely

    corroborate to VA medical staff that Plaintiff was suicidal, and further instructed

    Annie to abandon Plaintiff at the VA facility and direct the VA staff to detain and

    deal with him.
