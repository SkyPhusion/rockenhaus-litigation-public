17. The parties acquired certain marital property and assets during the course of the

    marriage. Plaintiff requests that the Court make a fair and equitable division of all

    marital property and debts pursuant to MCL 552 .19 et seq. Plaintiff acknowledges

    that some marital debt may exist and does not dispute that there are financial

    obligations to be fairly accounted for in these proceedings. However, Plaintiff

    specifically disputes Defendant's claim , made in her PPO petition , that Plaintiff

    owes her in excess of $100,000.00, including an alleged loan from her personal

    trust. Plaintiff submits that this figure is grossly inflated , unsupported by

    documentation that has been made available to Plaintiff, and was asserted in the

    PPO    petition   without the   supporting   financial   receipts   Defendant   herself

    acknowledged had not been attached . Plaintiff requests that the Court order a full

    and verified accounting of any alleged marital debts before any such claims are

    credited.
