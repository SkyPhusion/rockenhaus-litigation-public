21. Veterans Disability Compensation benefits are the separate, protected property of

    the veteran under federal law (38 U.S.C. § 5301) and are not subject to division as

    marital property. Defendant redirected six VA disability payments totaling

    $26,837.87 directly to her personal checking account during the period of October

    2025 through February 2026, while Plaintiff was incarcerated and unable to monitor

    or control his financial accounts. This diversion was made without Plaintiff's

    authorization and constitutes wrongful retention of federally protected veteran's

    benefits. Plaintiff requests that this Court order Defendant to return the full sum of

    $26,837.87 and to provide a complete accounting of how each payment was used.

22. Defendant fraudulently diverted five SSDI payments totaling $12 ,489.00 to her

    personal checking account between November 2025 and March 2026, while

    serving as Plaintiff's court-appointed Representative Payee and while falsely

    representing to Plaintiff and his appellate attorney that those payments had been

    suspended .   This   conduct    violates   42   U.S.C.   §   405(j)   and   constitutes

    Representative Payee fraud. This matter has been referred to the Social Security

    Administration Office of Inspector General. Additionally, the VA OIG is actively

    investigating under VA OIG Case No . 2026-18750, and the Ann Arbor Police

    Department is conducting a criminal investigation under AAPD Case No. 26-11892 .

    The combined total of VA and SSDI funds wrongfully diverted by Defendant is

    $39,326.87 . Plaintiff requests that this Court order Defendant to return all such

    funds in full and provide a sworn accounting of every payment received, retained,

    or spent on his behalf during the relevant period .
