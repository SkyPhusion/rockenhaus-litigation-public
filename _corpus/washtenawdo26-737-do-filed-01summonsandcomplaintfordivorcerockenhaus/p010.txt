16. Immediately after Plaintiff filed these protective motions, on February 12, 2026,

    Defendant sent a written communication directly to Plaintiff's appellate attorney

    explicitly stating that she was filing a divorce and PPO at 8:00 AM the following

    morning, and listing a series of allegations in threatening terms. That written

    communication constitutes a documented exhibit establishing that the PPO and the

    divorce filing were premeditated and scheduled, not a spontaneous response to

    fear. Defendant then filed her PPO against Plaintiff on February 20, 2026 and

    began making allegations of rape and sexual assault publicly and to Plaintiff's

    appellate attorney. Defendant also publicly amplified these same allegations across

    her social media platforms as part of the same retaliatory campaign. The sequence

    of events is unambiguous: Defendant's escalation, her abuse allegations, their

    public amplification on social media, and her PPO filing were all triggered directly

    by Plaintiff's lawful efforts to protect himself legally, and constitute a coordinated

    retaliatory pattern of conduct. The public nature of Defendant's social media

    campaign further demonstrates that the allegations were deployed for purposes of

    reputational harm and public pressure rather than genuine personal safety.
