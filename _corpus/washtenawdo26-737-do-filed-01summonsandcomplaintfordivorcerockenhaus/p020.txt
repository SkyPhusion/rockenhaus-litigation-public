23. On the day of Plaintiff's release from federal custody, March 2, 2026, Defendant

    took deliberate and coordinated steps to prevent Plaintiff from recovering his

    personal property from the marital residence at 26695 Ross Drive, Redford

    Township, Michigan. Specifically, Defendant made false statements to the Redford

    Township Police Department, falsely claiming that Plaintiff had never lived at the

    marital residence, in an effort to cause police to deny Plaintiff access to his own

    home and property. Additionally, Defendant solicited a mutual acquaintance, known

    to the parties as Annie, to corroborate these false statements to law enforcement.

    Plaintiff was completely denied access to the marital residence as a direct result of

    Defendant's false representations, leaving Plaintiff unable to retrieve his personal

    belongings, identification documents, clothing, and other property on the day he

    was released from incarceration with no housing, no funds, and no possessions . A

    police report documenting this incident exists and is incorporated herein by

    reference. Plaintiff further has text messages and other evidence documenting

    Defendant's solicitation of false statements from Annie.
