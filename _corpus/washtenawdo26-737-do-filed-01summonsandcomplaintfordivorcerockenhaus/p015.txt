Defendant's diversion of these SSDI funds constitutes fraud in her capacity as

Representative Payee. Federal law (42 U.S.C. § 405(j) and 20 C.F.R. § 404.2041)

requires that a Representative Payee use all benefits received solely for the

beneficiary's use and benefit. Defendant violated this obligation by redirecting

Plaintiff's SSDI funds to her personal account. This matter has been referred to the

Social Security Administration Office of Inspector General. The diversion of

Plaintiff's VA Disability Compensation funds is the subject of an active investigation

by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs Office of Inspector General (VA OIG

Case No. 2026-18750) . The Ann Arbor Police Department is conducting a parallel

criminal investigation (AAPD Case No. 26-11892).

Compounding this fraud, Defendant affirmatively misrepresented to both Plaintiff

and his appellate attorney that Plaintiff's SSDI payments had been suspended. This

false statement was made while Defendant was actively receiving and retaining

those same payments , preventing Plaintiff and his legal counsel from discovering

the diversion, seeking return of the funds, or taking corrective action . This

deliberate deception constitutes fraud        upon Plaintiff and    upon his legal

representation.
