      DEFENDANT'S FIRST SET OF REQUESTS FOR ADMISSIONS TO PLAINTIFF

NOW COMES Defendant, Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, appearing pro se, and pursuant to MCR 2.312,
requests that Plaintiff Adrienne Marjorie Rockenhaus admit or deny each of the following matters within
twenty-eight (28) days of service. Each matter not specifically denied shall be deemed admitted. Each denial
must fairly meet the substance of the request. If good faith requires qualification of an answer, the answer
shall specify so much of the matter as is true and qualify or deny the remainder.

                                               DEFINITIONS
1. "You" or "Your" means Adrienne Marjorie Rockenhaus.
2. "Conrad" or "Defendant" means Conrad Alan Rockenhaus.
3. "FCI Milan" means the Federal Correctional Institution located in Milan, Michigan.
4. "PPO" means the Personal Protection Order filed February 20, 2026, Case No. 26-102221-PP, Wayne
County Circuit Court.
5. "VA Benefits" means Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's VA Disability Compensation payments from the U.S.
Department of Veterans Affairs.
6. "SSDI Benefits" means Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's Social Security Disability Insurance payments from
the Social Security Administration.

                                      REQUESTS FOR ADMISSIONS
                                          I. VISITS TO FCI MILAN
REQUEST NO. 1: Admit or deny: You visited Conrad Alan Rockenhaus at FCI Milan on at least one
occasion.
REQUEST NO. 2: Admit or deny: You visited Conrad Alan Rockenhaus at FCI Milan on more than one
occasion.
REQUEST NO. 3: Admit or deny: You applied for and received visitation approval at FCI Milan in order to
visit Conrad Alan Rockenhaus.
REQUEST NO. 4: Admit or deny: You were present at FCI Milan as a visitor at any time during the period of
Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's incarceration there.
