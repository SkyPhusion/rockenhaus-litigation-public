     DEFENDANT/COUNTER-PLAINTIFF'S CHAIN OF CUSTODY SUPPLEMENT: SHA-256 HASH MANIFEST FOR MAY 12-13, 2026 SCRAPE
                                                     EVIDENCE

                                                                                I. PURPOSE
1. This Chain of Custody Supplement is filed in support of Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's Emergency Motion for Preservation Order and Order Prohibiting Spoliation of
   Evidence, filed contemporaneously herewith. It documents the cryptographic integrity of the public-record Twitter scrape datasets referenced in that Motion at §III.G ¶31
   ("the May 12, 2026 Scrape Evidence," used in the Motion and herein as a collective reference to the captures made on both May 12 and May 13, 2026).

                                                                      II. CAPTURE METHODOLOGY
2. The scrape datasets were captured between approximately 22:21 UTC on May 12, 2026 and 01:01 UTC on May 13, 2026 by Defendant using the Apify platform's tweet-
   scraper actor (https://apify.com). Each capture targeted specified X (formerly Twitter) account handles, conversation identifiers, or date ranges via the actor's input
   parameters. The actor returned aggregated tweet records as JSON arrays containing platform-native metadata fields including, but not limited to, tweet identifiers,
   conversation identifiers, in-reply-to identifiers, author profile information, timestamps in Twitter time format, source application identifiers, and user_mentions entities.
3. Each capture was saved as a single JSON file with a filename incorporating the targeted account or query subject and the UTC timestamp of capture completion. The files
   have not been modified after capture other than by file-naming. The contents are byte-for-byte identical to the data returned by the Apify actor.
