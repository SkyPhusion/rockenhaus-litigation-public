8. ORIGIN OF THE HANDWRITTEN NOTE LATER FILED AS RESPONDENT'S "EXHIBIT I". In or about
February or early March 2026, I received electronic communications on my own Google account that
purported on their face to be from Counter-Plaintiff. The content, tone, and circumstances of those
communications were not consistent with prior communications I had received from Counter-Plaintiff over
the course of our friendship. Separately, in the same general timeframe, I received a notification on my own
Google account indicating an unauthorized access attempt to my account. Based on my personal
observation of these two events, and based on my then-existing knowledge that Adrienne had access to
Counter-Plaintiff's Google account, I concluded at the time that the communications purporting to be from
Counter-Plaintiff had in fact been authored by Adrienne using her then-existing access to Counter-Plaintiff's
Google account, and that the access attempt against my own Google account had also been made by
Adrienne. I prepared a handwritten note at that time documenting these events. I am informed and believe
that a copy of this handwritten note was subsequently filed in the related personal protection order
proceeding by counsel for Adrienne as an exhibit. My purpose in writing the note was to document
Adrienne's unauthorized access conduct. The note was not written, and was not intended, to document any
conduct by Counter-Plaintiff. Any characterization of the note as evidence of Counter-Plaintiff's conduct
mischaracterizes both the content of the note and my purpose in writing it.
