      RESPONDENT'S NOTICE OF WITHDRAWAL OF MOTION FOR
         ISSUANCE OF SUBPOENA FOR WITNESS TESTIMONY

NOW COMES Respondent, Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, appearing pro se, and respectfully
gives notice of withdrawal of his Motion for Issuance of Subpoena for Witness Testimony,
currently calendared for hearing on July 21, 2026 at 10:00 AM via Zoom. In support of this
Notice, Respondent states as follows:

                               I. PROCEDURAL POSTURE
1. The Motion for Issuance of Subpoena for Witness Testimony was filed by Respondent for
   the limited and specific purpose of compelling witness testimony in defense of Petitioner's
   Motion to Show Cause for Violating Valid Personal Protection Order, which was set for
   hearing on April 29, 2026.
2. On April 29, 2026, the Honorable Yvonna C. Abraham, then presiding, dismissed
   Petitioner's Motion to Show Cause due to Petitioner's failure to appear at the duly noticed
   hearing.
3. By Order dated April 30, 2026, this Honorable Court reassigned all remaining pending
   motions in this matter to the PPO Docket. The reassignment order designates that a
   hearing in connection with the Motion for Issuance of Subpoena is presently calendared
   for July 21, 2026 at 10:00 AM via Zoom.

                                      II. MOOTNESS
4. The Motion for Issuance of Subpoena was directed solely toward securing witness
   testimony in defense of the now-dismissed Motion to Show Cause. With the dismissal of
   the Motion to Show Cause on April 29, 2026, the Motion for Issuance of Subpoena no
   longer serves any present procedural purpose in this matter.
5. The witnesses Respondent intends to call in support of his Motion to Modify, Extend, or
   Terminate Personal Protection Order and his Second Supplemental Motion to Modify,
   Extend, or Terminate Personal Protection Order include affiants who have voluntarily
   executed sworn statements and who, to Respondent's knowledge, are willing to appear
   voluntarily at any further proceedings without need for compulsory process.
