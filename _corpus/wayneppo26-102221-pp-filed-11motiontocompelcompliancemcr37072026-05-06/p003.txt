                                                  IV. AUTHORITY
25. The Rule. MCR 3.707(A)(2) provides that "the court must schedule and hold a hearing on a motion to modify or
    terminate a personal protection order within 14 days of the filing of the motion." The obligation is independently
    codified at MCL 600.2950(14) and MCL 600.2950a(14).
26. Appellate Authority. The Michigan Court of Appeals has confirmed that compliance with MCR 3.707(A)(2) is
    mandatory:
a. CAJ v KDT, 339 Mich App 459; 984 NW2d 504 (2021), reversed the trial court for failure to comply with MCR
3.707(A)(2), holding that the trial court was required to hold a hearing regarding the termination of the PPO.
b. JLS v HRS, ___ Mich App ___ (2024), reaffirmed that a respondent's motion to terminate a PPO triggers an
obligation on the part of the petitioner to establish a justification for continuance of the PPO at a hearing on the
respondent's motion.
c. HMM v JS, ___ Mich App ___ (2024), confirmed that the burden of proof at the merits hearing remains with the
petitioner throughout the proceeding.
27. Application. Respondent's Motion to Modify, Extend, or Terminate was filed within the timely-filing window of
    MCR 3.707(A)(1)(b) (ten days after actual notice; well within fourteen days). The mandatory hearing deadline
    under MCR 3.707(A)(2) was March 26, 2026. That deadline expired forty-one (41) days ago. The motion has been
    pending fifty-five (55) days. The Court retains no discretion to decline to schedule the merits hearing.
28. Scope of Relief. This Motion seeks compliance with the scheduling obligation. It does not seek substantive relief
