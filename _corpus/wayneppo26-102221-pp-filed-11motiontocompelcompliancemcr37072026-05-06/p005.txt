AFFIDAVIT IN SUPPORT OF MOTION TO COMPEL COMPLIANCE WITH MCR
                                3.707(A)(2)
STATE OF MICHIGAN )
            ) ss.
COUNTY OF WASHTENAW )
I, Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, being duly sworn, depose and state as follows:
 1. I am the Respondent in the above-captioned matter. I am over the age of eighteen and competent to testify to the
    matters set forth herein. The facts stated in this Affidavit are based on my personal knowledge.
 2. I am a one hundred percent (100%) service-connected disabled combat veteran of the United States Navy. I served
    deployments including operations in Kosovo (1999) with 1st Air Naval Gunfire Liaison Company (ANGLICO) and
    Operation Enduring Freedom (Afghanistan) with Radio Battalion. My service-connected disabilities include
    traumatic brain injury and seizure disorder.
 3. I was released from federal custody at FCI Milan on March 2, 2026. I received actual notice of the ex parte
    Personal Protection Order entered February 20, 2026 on that same date, March 2, 2026.
 4. On March 12, 2026, ten (10) days after I received actual notice of the PPO, I filed my Motion to Modify, Extend,
    or Terminate Personal Protection Order in this matter.
 5. On April 29, 2026, this Court conducted a Show Cause hearing on a separate motion filed by Petitioner. The April
    29 hearing was not, and was not scheduled as, the merits hearing on my Motion to Modify, Extend, or Terminate.
    To my knowledge, the merits hearing on my Motion to Terminate has never been scheduled.
 6. Among the medical devices prescribed to me by the Department of Veterans Affairs as part of my treatment
    regimen is an electrical-stimulation (e-stim) unit. The device is part of an established, VA-supervised therapy
    protocol for my service-connected conditions.
 7. The e-stim device is presently held at the marital residence to which the Personal Protection Order bars my access.
    I have been without access to this medical device since March 2, 2026, a period of sixty-five (65) days as of the
    filing of this Motion.
 8. On May 4, 2026, I filed a complaint with the Federal Trade Commission through IdentityTheft.gov regarding
    identity theft. The FTC has assigned Reference Number 201103951 to my matter. A copy of the FTC reference-
    number confirmation email is available for in-camera review or production should the Court require it.
 9. To file substantively complete disputes with creditors, financial institutions, and credit-reporting agencies under
    federal consumer-finance law, I require access to financial records (including bank statements, credit-card
    statements, account documentation, prior tax filings, and correspondence with financial institutions) that are
    presently held at the residence to which the Personal Protection Order bars my access.
10. The PPO no-contact framework prevents me from retrieving the records, directing a third party to retrieve them, or
    otherwise accessing them through the property-holder.
11. The deprivation of the e-stim medical device, the inaccessibility of the financial records required for active identity-
    theft remediation, and the broader property-deprivation harms previously documented in my pending Motion to
    Modify, Extend, or Terminate and my Motion for Court Supervised Access to Property are not speculative. They
    are concrete, ongoing, and continue to accrue each day the merits hearing on my Motion to Terminate remains
    unscheduled.
12. I make this Affidavit in support of Respondent's Motion to Compel Compliance with MCR 3.707(A)(2) and to
    Schedule Hearing on Pending Motion to Modify, Extend, or Terminate Personal Protection Order.
