19. PHOTOGRAPH SENT TO PETITIONER DURING INCARCERATION: I sent Petitioner a

photograph of myself during my incarceration, accompanied by a handwritten note expressing

2026.

20. PETITIONER'S FORWARD ING OF MAIL AFTER PPO ISSUANCE: On or about April 4,

2026, after my release and after the PPO had been entered, I received at the Delonis Center an envelope

that Petitioner had affirmatively forwarded to me. The envelope contained the photograph and note I

had sent her during my incarceration. Petitioner's act of locating my post-release address and

forwarding mail to me while simultaneously maintaining an active PPO against me is inconsistent with

any genuine fear of contact with me.

21. TENOR OF PETITIONER'S TRULINCS CORRESPONDENCE:                                   The TRULINCS

correspondence between Petitioner and me during my incarceration included messages from Petitioner

to me such as the October 28, 2025 message (contained within the TRULINCS records) in which

Petitioner closed by saying "I love you but I require a calm, supportive and safe environment." The

tenor of Petitioner's messages to me during this period is inconsistent with a person in fear of the

addressee.

22. LACK OF KNOWLEDGE OF PPO UNTIL MARCH 2, 2026: I did not know that Petitioner

had filed a Personal Protection Order Petition against me until March 2, 2026. I was not served with the

Petition or with the Ex Parte PPO while in federal custody. I first learned of the PPO on March 2, 2026,

when, shortly after my release from FCI Milan that morning, I traveled to the residence at 26695 Ross

Drive, Redford, Michigan, to retrieve personal property, and a neighbor present at the scene and the

responding Redford Police Department officers informed me that a PPO existed.
