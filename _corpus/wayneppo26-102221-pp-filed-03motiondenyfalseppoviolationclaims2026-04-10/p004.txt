FALSE, HER OWN POSTS PROVE THE SIGNAL ACCOUNT WAS AN

22. In addition to the burner phone claims , Petitioner publicly alleged on or
around March 27-29, 2026 that Respondent contacted her via Signal and
arranged for an unsolicited Domino's Pizza delivery to her residence. Both
claims are false and are refuted by Petitioner's own social media posts.
23. Regarding the Signal claim: Petitioner herself published a screenshot of the
Signal interface showing an incoming message from an account named 'Dan
Ferris.' The Signal application prominently displayed a 'Review Carefully'
warning stating: 'Profile names are not verified' and 'No groups in common,' and
prompted Petitioner to Block, Report, or Accept the message. This warning
