Description:
Two documents included as components of this exhibit. Component 1: Complete and unredacted text
of the March 4, 2026 4:17 AM email from Respondent Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, sending address
conradrockenhausa2@gmail.com (Respondent's then-active personal email address; Respondent
has since retired this address and now uses crockenhaus@icloud.com as his primary email), sent
simultaneously to two recipients: Rob Hein at rob.hein@qolity.com (Petitioner's nephew) and Ann
Helgren at anniehelgren@gmail.com (Petitioner's longstanding friend, see Exhibit 15). The email
was forwarded to Respondent in original form, with full headers preserved, by co-recipient Ann Helgren
on April 25, 2026. Component 2: Notice of Change of Address filed by Respondent in pro per in
Rockenhaus v. Stylianos Agapiou et al., U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan, Case
No. 2:25-cv-12716, Hon. Jane M. Beckering, ECF No. 28, filed March 9, 2026, identifying
conradrockenhausa2@gmail.com as Respondent's then-active email address of record with that
federal court.

Exhibit J in support of her Motion to Show Cause. Petitioner's Exhibit J is incomplete: the two

crockenhaus@icloud.com as his primary email). It was not a “burner.” Component 2 of this exhibit is the
Notice of Change of Address filed by Respondent in pro per in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan on March 9, 2026, five days after the email Petitioner mischaracterizes, explicitly
identifying the same conradrockenhausa2@gmail.com address as Respondent's address of record with
