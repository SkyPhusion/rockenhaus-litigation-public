                                XIII. CRYPTOCURRENCY AND FUNDRAISING
INTERROGATORY NO. 36: State whether you organized, participated in, promoted, or received proceeds
from any cryptocurrency fundraiser, token sale, crowdfunding campaign, donation drive, Patreon account,
GoFundMe, or any other fundraising activity related to Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, his case, his incarceration,
or any cause associated with him or his name. If so, state: (a) the nature of the fundraising activity; (b) the
platform or mechanism used; (c) the dates the fundraising was active; (d) the total amount raised; (e) how
the funds were received and into what account; (f) how the funds were used or disbursed; and (g) whether
Conrad Alan Rockenhaus was aware of or consented to the fundraising activity.

                                        XIV. FAMILY COMMUNICATIONS
INTERROGATORY NO. 37: Identify all communications you had with Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's mother,
Lenita Rockenhaus, or any other member of his family, from the date of his incarceration through the
present. For each communication, state: (a) the date; (b) the method of communication; (c) the substance of
the communication; and (d) whether the information you conveyed to his family members was accurate and
complete, or whether you withheld or misrepresented any information regarding Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's
circumstances, legal proceedings, finances, or relationship with you.

These Interrogatories are continuing in nature and require supplemental responses if additional responsive
information becomes available.
