INTERROGATORY NO. 21: State how you came to know that Conrad Alan Rockenhaus was designated as
a Care Level 4 inmate by the Federal Bureau of Prisons. Specifically state: (a) when you first learned of his
Care Level 4 designation; (b) from whom or from what source you obtained this information; (c) whether that
person or source was authorized to disclose his BOP medical classification to you; (d) whether you solicited
any BOP employee, medical staff member, or other person to provide you with his care level designation or
any other medical classification information; (e) how you came to know that he had uncontrolled seizures
while in BOP custody; (f) the source of your information regarding the nature, frequency, or severity of his
seizure disorder while incarcerated; (g) whether you filed, submitted, or communicated any complaints,
concerns, or reports to the BOP, FCI Milan, or any BOP medical staff regarding Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's
medical condition, care, or treatment, whether solicited or unsolicited; and (h) whether you solicited any
other person to contact BOP staff on your behalf or on Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's behalf regarding his
medical condition or care level.
INTERROGATORY NO. 22: Identify all of Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's private email accounts, digital
accounts, cloud storage accounts, or online platforms that you accessed, monitored, or from which you
extracted data at any time, including but not limited to accounts at rockenhaus.com, cannabytes.net,
icloud.com, gmail.com, or any VA or SSA online portal. For each account, state: (a) the account; (b) the
dates of access; (c) how access was obtained; and (d) what data was extracted or reviewed.

                                        VIII. POWER OF ATTORNEY
INTERROGATORY NO. 23: Identify every action you took, communication you made, transaction you
executed, account you accessed, document you signed, representation you made, or authority you
exercised under or purportedly under the Power of Attorney granted to you by Conrad Alan Rockenhaus
while he was incarcerated at FCI Milan or any other federal facility. For each action, state: (a) the date; (b)
the nature of the action; (c) the person or institution with whom you communicated or transacted; (d)
whether you identified yourself as acting under the Power of Attorney; and (e) the outcome or result of the
action.
INTERROGATORY NO. 24: Identify every communication you sent to or received from any person or entity,
including but not limited to family members, friends, attorneys, medical providers, government agencies,
courts, law enforcement, financial institutions, social workers, employers, media, blog readers, social media
followers, or any other third party, regarding Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's incarceration, his federal criminal
case, his federal civil cases, his VA or SSDI benefits, his medical or psychiatric condition, his finances, his
release, or his post-release circumstances, from the date of his incarceration through the present. For each
communication, state: (a) the date; (b) the method of communication; (c) the identity of the recipient or
sender; (d) the subject matter; and (e) the substance of the communication.
INTERROGATORY NO. 25: Identify every communication you sent to or received from the Federal Bureau
of Prisons, FCI Milan, Livingston County Jail, the United States Department of Justice, the United States
Courts, or any employee, officer, or agent of any of those institutions, regarding Conrad Alan Rockenhaus at
any time. For each communication, state: (a) the date; (b) the method of communication; (c) the name and
title of the person you communicated with; (d) the subject matter; and (e) the complete substance of the
communication, including any requests you made, any information you provided, and any response you
received.
INTERROGATORY NO. 26: Identify every letter, card, note, photograph, drawing, or other piece of physical
correspondence sent to you by Conrad Alan Rockenhaus from FCI Milan, Livingston County Jail, or any
other federal or county facility during his incarceration. For each item, state: (a) the approximate date
received; (b) a description of the item; (c) whether you still possess the item; and (d) if you no longer
possess it, when and how it was disposed of.
