                                   XI. TRUST ACCOUNT AND ROB HEIN
INTERROGATORY NO. 30: Identify the trust referenced in your Petition for Personal Protection Order in
which you claimed Conrad Alan Rockenhaus owes you over $100,000 including a formally documented
loan. State: (a) the full legal name of the trust; (b) the date the trust was created; (c) the name of the trustee
or trustees; (d) the name of any co-trustees, managers, or administrators, including Rob Hein; (e) the
financial institution where the trust account is held, including the bank name, branch, and account number;
(f) the current balance of the trust; (g) every disbursement made from the trust from the date of the parties'
marriage through the present, including the date, amount, purpose, and recipient of each disbursement; (h)
every disbursement from the trust used for any marital purpose, including rent, legal fees, living expenses,
or any other shared expense; and (i) the basis for your claim that Conrad Alan Rockenhaus owes you
$100,000 or any other amount from the trust.
INTERROGATORY NO. 31: Identify every disbursement from your trust that was used for any purpose
related to the marriage, including but not limited to rent payments, legal fees, living expenses, travel, or any
other marital expense. For each disbursement, state: (a) the date; (b) the amount; (c) the purpose; (d)
whether Conrad Alan Rockenhaus was aware of the disbursement; (e) whether the disbursement was
characterized as a loan, a gift, or a marital contribution; and (f) whether any written documentation of the
disbursement exists.
INTERROGATORY NO. 32: Identify the attorney or attorneys whose fees were paid in whole or in part
using funds from your trust during the marriage. For each payment, state: (a) the attorney's name and firm;
(b) the amount paid; (c) the date of payment; (d) the matter for which the attorney was retained; (e) whether
Conrad Alan Rockenhaus expressed a desire to repay the trust for this payment; and (f) whether that
repayment obligation was ever documented in writing.
INTERROGATORY NO. 33: Identify every distribution, payment, income, or financial benefit you received
from your trust from the date of the parties' marriage through the present, including the date, amount, and
purpose of each. State whether any of these distributions were disclosed to the Social Security
Administration in connection with your role as Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's Representative Payee, or to any
other government agency.
INTERROGATORY NO. 34: Describe Rob Hein's role in managing your trust, including: (a) his title or
capacity with respect to the trust; (b) the nature and scope of his management responsibilities; (c) any
compensation he receives for managing the trust; (d) any communications between you and Rob Hein
regarding Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, this divorce proceeding, or any related legal matter; and (e) whether
Rob Hein has any financial interest in the outcome of this divorce proceeding.
