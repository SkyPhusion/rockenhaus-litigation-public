                                  VI. PPO AND RELATED PROCEEDINGS
INTERROGATORY NO. 15: Identify all communications between you and any person other than your
retained legal counsel regarding your decision to file the Personal Protection Order (Case No. 26-102221-
PP) on February 20, 2026, including the timing of the decision relative to Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's filing of
his Motion to Sever on February 18, 2026. This interrogatory does not seek communications protected by
the attorney-client privilege; however, identify by date, recipient, and general subject matter any
communications with your attorney(s) regarding this decision that you are withholding on privilege grounds.
INTERROGATORY NO. 16: Identify all communications you sent to or received from Conrad Alan
Rockenhaus's federal appellate attorney between January 1, 2026 and March 2, 2026, including but not
limited to the February 9, 2026 email and the February 12, 2026 email.
INTERROGATORY NO. 17: Identify every incident of alleged abuse, harassment, or threatening conduct by
Conrad Alan Rockenhaus that you relied upon in support of your Petition for Personal Protection Order. For
each incident, state: (a) the date; (b) the location; (c) the nature of the conduct; (d) whether any police report
was filed; and (e) whether any medical treatment was sought.
INTERROGATORY NO. 18: Identify every source from which you obtained Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's
private records, communications, medical information, prescription records, or digital account data that was
submitted or referenced in any judicial proceeding, including the PPO petition and any response to the
Motion to Terminate PPO. For each item of information, state: (a) what the information was; (b) how you
obtained it; (c) the date you obtained it; (d) whether you had authorization to access it; and (e) in which
proceeding(s) it was used.
INTERROGATORY NO. 19: State whether you contacted the Federal Bureau of Prisons or FCI Milan at any
time to request, obtain, or access Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's medical records, mental health records,
psychiatric records, medication records, or any other protected health information. If so, state: (a) the date of
each request or contact; (b) the name and title of the BOP or FCI Milan employee you contacted; (c) the
method of contact; (d) what specific records or information you requested; (e) what authority or basis you
claimed for the request; (f) whether you identified yourself as his Power of Attorney, Representative Payee,
next friend, spouse, or in any other capacity; (g) what records or information were provided to you; (h) what
you did with those records after receiving them; and (i) to whom, if anyone, you disclosed those records or
any information contained in them.
INTERROGATORY NO. 20: State whether you added any person to a YouTube Premium family plan,
Google One family plan, Apple Family Sharing plan, or any other shared subscription service account at any
time during the marriage. If so, state: (a) the name of each person added; (b) the date each person was
added; (c) the subscription service involved; (d) whether the account was paid for using marital funds or
funds belonging to Conrad Alan Rockenhaus; (e) the nature of your relationship with each person added;
and (f) whether Conrad Alan Rockenhaus was aware of or consented to the addition of each person.
