             III. PART TWO: MOTION FOR FINANCIAL DISCLOSURE AND ACCOUNTING
11. MCR 3.206(C) requires each party in a domestic relations action to provide the other party with a verified
    financial disclosure statement. Plaintiff has not provided any such disclosure despite the case being at
    issue.
12. MCL 552.401 authorizes this Court to make orders as it considers just and equitable concerning the
    disposition of property in light of a party's dissipation of marital assets. Plaintiff's diversion of
    approximately $39,326.87 in Defendant's federal disability benefits constitutes precisely the dissipation
    of assets MCL 552.401 is designed to address.
13. VA Payment History records document that VA Disability Compensation payments ranging from $4,317
    to $4,764 per month were directed to accounts under Plaintiff's control during the period of September
    2025 through February 2026, totaling approximately $26,837.87, without Defendant's authorization.
14. In her capacity as SSA-appointed Representative Payee, Plaintiff received five SSDI payments totaling
    $12,489.00 between November 2025 and March 2026 that she was legally required under 42 U.S.C.
    §405(j) to use solely for Defendant's benefit. She did not. She retained them while falsely telling
    Defendant and his appellate attorney that his SSDI had been suspended.
15. Plaintiff has continued to operate Cannabytes, LLC, a business in which Defendant holds the title of Vice
    President of Systems Engineering and is a formally designated corporate officer, while completely
    excluding Defendant from all operations, accounts, and revenues since the PPO was filed. No
    accounting has been provided.
16. The urgency of this motion is heightened by the fact that Plaintiff has had sole control over approximately
    $39,326.87 in Defendant's funds since September 2025, plus Cannabytes, LLC revenues. Those funds
    may be further dissipated if disclosure and accounting are not ordered immediately.
