                   II. PART ONE: MOTION FOR ORDER TO PRESERVE EVIDENCE
 7. Michigan courts have inherent authority to issue preservation orders to protect the integrity of evidence in
    pending litigation. MCR 2.310 and MCR 2.313 authorize this Court to enter orders protecting the
    evidentiary record.
 8. The duty to preserve evidence is in full effect. This case is active and pending. Defendant has today
    served Plaintiff with a comprehensive discovery package consisting of Interrogatories, Requests for
    Production, and Requests for Admissions. The evidence sought in that discovery package is directly
    relevant to the claims and defenses in this action.
 9. Spoliation of evidence is sanctionable under Michigan law and may give rise to an adverse inference
    instruction at trial. Teel v. Meredith, 284 Mich App 412 (2009). Plaintiff went silent on all social media
    platforms immediately upon retaining counsel, suggesting awareness of the evidentiary significance of
    her public posts advocating for Defendant's release, posts that are directly contradicted by her PPO
    allegations.
10. The following categories of evidence must be immediately preserved:

                                             A. Financial Records
All bank statements, transaction records, and financial records relating to Plaintiff's receipt, retention,
transfer, or disbursement of Defendant's VA Disability Compensation and SSDI benefits from September
2025 through the present, including all personal accounts, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, cryptocurrency
wallets, and any other financial account into which Defendant's funds were deposited.

                                     B. Digital and Electronic Evidence
All emails, text messages, social media posts, blog posts, website content, TRULINCS messages, online
portal access logs, id.me account access logs, va.gov account access records, and any other electronic
communications or digital records relating to Defendant, his incarceration, his benefits, his medical
condition, his finances, his release, or any claims or defenses in this action.

                                        C. Physical Correspondence
All letters, cards, notes, photographs, drawings, envelopes, and any other physical correspondence sent by
Plaintiff to Defendant at FCI Milan or any other facility during his incarceration, including the November 2025
handwritten letter, Priority Mail Express packages sent by Plaintiff to Defendant, and the photograph with
accompanying note that Plaintiff forwarded to the Robert J. Delonis Center after the PPO was filed.
