6. Counterclaim ¶50, rockenhaus.com registration date. Counterclaim ¶50 states that the

III. CORRECTION TO DEFENDANT'S OMNIBUS THREE-LAYER MOTION (FILED APRIL 16, 2026)
7. Omnibus ¶14, SSDI overpayment period. Omnibus ¶14 characterizes the SSDI overpayment period as
"November 2025 and March 2026." The Social Security Administration's March 12, 2026 demand letter
(BNC No. 26D1153B02352-A) addressed to Plaintiff as Defendant's Representative Payee identifies the
overpayment period as "10/2025 to 03/2026." Omnibus ¶14 shall be read as: "October 2025 through March
2026," conforming to the SSA's own characterization of the overpayment period.
