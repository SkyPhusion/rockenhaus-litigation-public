 TO: Plaintiff Adrienne Marjorie Rockenhaus, self-represented.
 PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that the following eleven (11) motions of Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff Conrad Alan
 Rockenhaus will be brought on for hearing before the Honorable Nicole N. Goodson, Third Judicial Circuit
 Court for the County of Wayne, Family Division, on August 26, 2026 at 11:30 a.m., or as soon thereafter as
 the parties may be heard:

 1. Motion for Leave to Exceed Interrogatories;
 2. Motion for Issuance of Subpoenas;
 3. Omnibus Motion (Preservation of Evidence; Financial Disclosure; rockenhaus.com Domain Temporary
    Restraining Order);
 4. Motion for Temporary Restraining Order Prohibiting Dissipation of the USAA Account Receiving Diverted
    Federal Veterans and Social Security Benefits;
 5. Motion to Restrict Access to Protected Personal Identifying Information on Filed Exhibits and to File
    Redacted Replacement Exhibits;
 6. Motion for Return of Personal Property, Restoration of Electronic Account Access, Inventory and
    Accounting, and Court-Supervised Retrieval Mechanism;
 7. Motion for Preservation Order and Order Prohibiting Spoliation of Evidence;
 8. Motion for Leave to File First Amended Counterclaim;
 9. Motion to Determine Sufficiency of Answers to Requests for Admissions, to Compel Responses to
    Interrogatories and Requests for Production of Documents, and for Discovery Sanctions;
10. Motion for Default Judgment on Counterclaim; and
11. Motion to Inquire into Plaintiff's Capacity and to Appoint a Guardian Ad Litem for Plaintiff.
 Motions 1-10 were calendared for the above date and time by ePraecipe accepted by the Court on June 10,
 2026. Motion 11 was calendared for the above date and time by ePraecipe accepted by the Court on July
 28, 2026.
 APPEARANCE: This hearing will be conducted in person at the Coleman A. Young Municipal Center
 (CAYMC), 2 Woodward Avenue, Detroit, Michigan 48226, in the courtroom of the Honorable Nicole N.
 Goodson, Room 1501. This Notice amends and supersedes the Zoom appearance instructions set forth in
