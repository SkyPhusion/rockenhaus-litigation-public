13. DENIAL OF ALLEGED CORRLINKS ABUSE: I did not send Petitioner barrages of abusive or

demanding messages via the BOP's TRULINCS/CorrLinks system during my incarceration. The actual

correspondence pattern during my incarceration is the opposite of what the Petition alleges. The

overwhelming majority of TRULINCS correspondence consisted of affectionate messages from

Petitioner to me, along with letters and at least one Priority Mail Express package sent by her to me at

FCI Milan. The TRULINCS records, which I have subpoenaed from the Bureau of Prisons, will
confirm this pattern.

14. DENIAL OF PROXY STALKING: I did not direct any person, including anyone named "Jerome

Sallad," to call or text Petitioner on October 13, 2025 or on any other date. I did not have a telephone

during my federal custody. I had no means to direct third parties to contact Petitioner.

15. DENIAL OF 5/4/2023 PHONE-GRAB FACIAL-SCRATCH ALLEGATION: I did not scratch

Petitioner's face on May 4, 2023. I did not delete any photograph from Petitioner's phone. The Petition's

embellishment of the May 4, 2023 Plymouth incident is contradicted by Petitioner's own medical

exhibit (the VA Emergency Department record), which documents neither a facial scratch nor a deleted

photograph.

16. DENIAL OF 2024 ALLEGATIONS AND GENERAL DENIAL: I was not charged with any
offense arising from the incidents Petitioner describes in the Petition. No police report referenced in the

Petition resulted in my arrest, charging, or conviction. To the extent the Petition contains additional

factual allegations of violence, threats, stalking, or harassment by me against Petitioner that are not

specifically addressed above, I deny them. I have never physically assaulted, stalked, or harassed

Petitioner.

17. COMMUNICATION CHANNELS DURING CUSTODY: During my federal custody at FCI

Milan from September 4, 2025 through March 2, 2026, I communicated with Petitioner through only

two channels: the BOP's TRULINCS electronic messaging system and U.S. Mail. These are the only

communication channels available to federal inmates for contact with persons outside the institution.
