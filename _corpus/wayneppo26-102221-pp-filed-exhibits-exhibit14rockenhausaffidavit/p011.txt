3. MARCH 2026 VA DISABILITY PAYMENT: The U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
disburses my monthly disability compensation on the first business day of the month for which
the benefit is owed, pursuant to 38 C.F.R. § 3.31. Where the first calendar day of a month falls
on a weekend or federal holiday, the VA advances the payment to the immediately preceding
business day. March 1, 2026 fell on a Sunday. The VA accordingly disbursed my March 2026
monthly disability payment on Friday, February 27, 2026. The line item on Exhibit 11 (VA
Payment History) showing a pay date of February 27, 2026 is the March 2026 monthly
payment, not a February 2026 payment. That March 2026 payment was deposited into a
USAA account under Petitioner's control pursuant to the Power of Attorney that I had revoked
by certified mail effective February 17, 2026 (Exhibit 3) . Upon my release from federal custody
on March 2, 2026, I requested that Petitioner release the March 2026 payment to me.
Petitioner refused. I subsequently sought third-party assistance from Rob Hein and Ann
Helgren in recovering the funds, as documented in Exhibit 18.
