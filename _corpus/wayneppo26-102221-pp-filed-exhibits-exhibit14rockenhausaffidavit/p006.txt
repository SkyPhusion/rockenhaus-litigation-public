24. LACK OF KNOWLEDGE OF VA DISABILITY COMPENSATION DIVERSION UNTIL
ON OR ABOUT FEBRUARY 9, 2026: I did not know that Petitioner had diverted my VA disability
compensation to her personal USAA account until on or about February 9, 2026. Petitioner's email of

February 9, 2026 to my federal appellate counsel, Assistant Federal Public Defender Kaycee Berente

(PPO Hearing Bundle Exhibit l ), was the first communication in which Petitioner made clear her

intention to leave me with "no money, no bank account, no home to return to" upon release. The

February 9, 2026 email, combined with my prior awareness that the joint SoFi account had been closed,

caused me to understand in February 2026 that Petitioner was retaining my VA benefits rather than

holding them for my use. Prior to February 9, 2026, I had no knowledge that Petitioner had
affirmatively redirected my VA benefits to her own personal account. Petitioner had previously

represented to me via TRULINCS on October 28, 2025 that "VA pay has not been deposited," a

representation that was false and was made while Petitioner was in fact receiving those deposits to her

personal USAA account.

25. LACK OF KNOWLEDGE OF SSDI DIVERSION UNTIL MARCH 10, 2026: I did not know

that Petitioner had diverted my Social Security Disability Insurance payments to her personal account

until March 10, 2026. Prior to that date, Petitioner had represented to me, and to my federal counsel,

that my SSDI payments had been "suspended." This representation was false . Petitioner was in fact

receiving my SSDI payments as my court-appointed Representative Payee and retaining them for her

personal use rather than disbursing them for my benefit. I first learned that the SSDI payments were not

suspended, but had been diverted, when I was able to access SSDI records after my release and

determined the true status of the payments on March 10, 2026.
