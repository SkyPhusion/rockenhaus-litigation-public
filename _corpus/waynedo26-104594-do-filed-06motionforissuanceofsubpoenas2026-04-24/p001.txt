                 DEFENDANT'S MOTION FOR ISSUANCE OF SUBPOENAS

NOW COMES Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff pro se in Case No. 26-104594-DO
and Respondent pro se in Case No. 26-102221-PP, and respectfully moves this Court for an Order directing
the Clerk to issue subpoenas for discovery in the divorce proceeding and witness testimony in the Personal
Protection Order hearing, pursuant to MCR 2.305, MCR 2.506, and this Court's local practices. In support
thereof, Defendant states as follows:

                                         I. STATEMENT OF FACTS
1. This matter involves parallel proceedings: a divorce action (Case No. 26-104594-DO) and a Personal
   Protection Order petition (Case No. 26-102221-PP), both pending before this Court.
2. Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff seeks to conduct discovery relevant to marital asset identification, financial
   misconduct, and matters material to property division in the divorce proceeding.
3. Respondent requires witness testimony for the Personal Protection Order hearing currently scheduled for
   April 29, 2026.
4. The requested subpoenas are necessary to obtain relevant, admissible evidence that is proportional to
   the issues in dispute and not readily available through other means.

                                           II. LEGAL AUTHORITY
This Motion is brought pursuant to:

   MCR 2.305(A)(1), permitting discovery regarding any matter relevant to the subject matter of the pending
   action;
   MCR 2.506, authorizing subpoenas for documents and testimony; and
   MCR 2.305(A)(2), requiring service on counsel of record pending resolution of withdrawal motions.
