7. THE MAY 4, 2026 FACEBOOK POST NAMING ME. On or about May 4, 2026, I learned of a Facebook
post published under Counter-Plaintiff Conrad Rockenhaus's account containing language characterized in
the post as a "[SYSTEM ALERT] DEAD MAN'S SWITCH." The post named five persons whom the post
identified as "responsible for [Conrad Rockenhaus's] death." I was named in that list. Learning that my own
name had been published in a list identifying persons "responsible for [his] death" was distressing to me. I
had no involvement of any kind in the publication of that post. I have no knowledge or reason to believe that
Counter-Plaintiff authored or authorized the post; I understand the post to have been published while his
Facebook account was compromised.
8. MY PARTICIPATION IN THE MAY 5, 2026 REDFORD POLICE DEPARTMENT WALK-IN. On May 5,
2026, I was present at the Redford Township Police Department along with Counter-Plaintiff and my sister
Ann Helgren to make an in-person report concerning the Facebook post described in paragraph 7 above
and related concerns. Counter-Plaintiff was the primary reporting party. I corroborated the matters reported
as a witness, based on my personal knowledge that I was named in the May 4, 2026 Facebook post as
described in paragraph 7. The walk-in was received by Officer Young (badge #192) of the Redford
Township Police Department. The matter was assigned a Redford Police Department case number, which I
understand to be Case 26-7649.
9. THE SATURDAY MAY 9, 2026 EVENING VACATION OF 26695 ROSS DRIVE. On the evening of
Saturday, May 9, 2026, I personally observed activity at 26695 Ross Drive, Redford Township, Michigan,
from my own residence. I personally observed an unknown adult male and a white pickup truck at 26695
Ross Drive during the loading of household property from the residence into the truck. I did not personally
observe Adrienne Marjorie Rockenhaus during this activity. I personally observed photographs taken by my
sister Ann Helgren that same evening that depicted the same activity I observed.
10. THE SUNDAY MAY 10, 2026 EVENING SECOND TRIP TO 26695 ROSS DRIVE. On the evening of
Sunday, May 10, 2026, I personally observed additional activity at 26695 Ross Drive, Redford Township,
Michigan, from my own residence. I observed two adult females present at 26695 Ross Drive for a period of
approximately one hour during the evening. From my line-of-sight position I was not able to identify the
vehicle they used to arrive or depart. One of the two females was a brunette. I am not able to confirm
whether either of the two females I observed was Adrienne Marjorie Rockenhaus.
