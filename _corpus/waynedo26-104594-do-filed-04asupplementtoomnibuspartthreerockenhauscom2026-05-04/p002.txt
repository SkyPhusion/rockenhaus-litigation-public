II. SUPPLEMENTAL FACTUAL CATEGORIES
                     A. False-Swearing Misuse: The February 20, 2026 PPO Petition

 5. On February 20, 2026, Plaintiff filed an ex parte Petition for Personal Protection Order (Domestic
    Relationship) in 26-102221-PP. The Petition contained an attached "Relief Requested - Other Relief"
    page on which Plaintiff swore, under penalty of perjury, the following statement:

          "As the owner of a digital software and web business (Cannabytes, LLC), my 'workplace'
          consists of my digital infrastructure, servers, and domains (including cannabytes.net and
          rockenhaus.com). The Respondent has recently weaponized my workplace infrastructure to
          stalk me. ... I am requesting the court explicitly prohibit the Respondent from accessing,
          interfering with, or utilizing my business domains and prohibit him from contacting any clients or
          impairing my digital work environment in any way."

 6. The sworn assertion that rockenhaus.com is one of Plaintiff's "business domains" or part of her
    "workplace" is false. The WHOIS record for rockenhaus.com, attached to the April 16, 2026 Omnibus
    Motion as Exhibit A, conclusively establishes the domain's creation date as September 7, 2001, more
    than twenty-one (21) years before the parties' marriage on May 20, 2023, and three years before
    Plaintiff's own LARA filings show the formation of Cannabytes, LLC.
 7. Plaintiff used this sworn false statement to obtain ex parte court orders prohibiting Defendant from
    "accessing" his own premarital separate-property domain, a domain that predates the marriage by more
    than two decades.
 8. The misuse documented at paragraphs 5–7 is not historical. It is an ongoing operative misuse: Plaintiff
    continues to assert exclusive control over rockenhaus.com on the basis of, and in reliance upon, the
    same sworn statement she filed on February 20, 2026. The PPO order entered on the basis of that
    sworn statement remains in force today, more than ten weeks after entry, and continues to reference
    Plaintiff's "business domains" as the property she is entitled to protect from Defendant's "interference."
 9. Plaintiff's continued assertion that rockenhaus.com is hers, directly contradicting the WHOIS record
    establishing Defendant's twenty-one-year premarital ownership, is itself a form of misuse warranting the
    structural protection requested below. It is also pattern evidence of bad faith with this Court system that
    the Court may consider in assessing the balance-of-hardships factor under Pontiac Fire Fighters.

                    B. Lockout Misuse: The conrad@rockenhaus.com Email Account
10. Defendant has used the email address conrad@rockenhaus.com as his professional email of record
    continuously from on or about September 2001 through the date of his federal incarceration. The
    account predates the marriage by approximately twenty-two (22) years.
11. While Defendant was incarcerated at FCI Milan and unable to access his own digital infrastructure,
    Plaintiff changed the administrative credentials for the rockenhaus.com mail configuration and locked
    Defendant out of the conrad@rockenhaus.com email account.
12. The lockout was documented contemporaneously in Defendant's affidavit filed in the federal district court
    at Rockenhaus v. Agapiou, E.D. Mich. Docket No. 388083, Document 30. Defendant has since regained
    limited functional access to his communications through other means but remains locked out of his own
    surname-domain professional email account, which Plaintiff continues to administratively control.
13. Each day the lockout continues is a day Defendant is unable to:

    correspondence from third parties (clients, vendors, federal agencies, financial institutions) who may not
    have his current alternative addresses;
