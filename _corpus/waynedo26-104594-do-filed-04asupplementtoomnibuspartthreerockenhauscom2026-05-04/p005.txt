STATE OF MICHIGAN )

I, Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, being duly sworn, depose and state as follows:
1. I am the Defendant in the above-captioned matter. I am over the age of eighteen and competent to
   testify to the matters set forth herein. The facts stated in this Affidavit are based on my personal
   knowledge except where stated on information and belief.
2. I registered rockenhaus.com on or about September 7, 2001. The domain has been continuously
   registered to me since that date, more than twenty-one (21) years before my marriage to Plaintiff on May
   20, 2023. The WHOIS record attached as Exhibit A to my April 16, 2026 Omnibus Motion documents the
   September 7, 2001 creation date.
3. Cannabytes, LLC was formed in approximately 2022. I am familiar with the formation and operating
   history of Cannabytes, LLC, including from LARA annual statements that I personally signed as Member
   of the LLC. Cannabytes, LLC did not own rockenhaus.com at any point in its existence and does not own
   rockenhaus.com now. rockenhaus.com is my pre-marital separate property.
4. I have used the email address conrad@rockenhaus.com as my professional email of record continuously
   from on or about September 2001 through approximately September 2025, when I was taken into federal
   custody. The address has been used by me to correspond with clients, vendors, federal agencies,
   banks, credit-card issuers, and other entities for whom the address remains the email of record on file.
5. While I was incarcerated at FCI Milan and unable to administer my own digital infrastructure, Plaintiff
   changed the administrative credentials for the rockenhaus.com mail infrastructure and locked me out of
   my conrad@rockenhaus.com email account. I do not have administrative access to that account today.
6. I have testified to the lockout previously in my affidavit filed at Rockenhaus v. Agapiou, E.D. Mich.
   Docket No. 388083, Document 30. The factual content of that prior affidavit, regarding the lockout, is true
   and correct and is repeated here.
7. I have read the February 20, 2026 ex parte Petition for Personal Protection Order filed by Plaintiff in 26-
   102221-PP, including the attached "Relief Requested - Other Relief" page. The Petition is signed by
   Plaintiff under penalty of perjury.
8. The Petition's assertion that rockenhaus.com is one of Plaintiff's "business domains" or part of her
   "workplace" is false. The WHOIS record establishes my pre-marital ownership as a matter of objective
   public record. I did not transfer ownership of rockenhaus.com to Plaintiff at any point during the marriage.
   I did not transfer ownership to Cannabytes, LLC at any point during the marriage. My pre-marital
   separate-property interest in the domain has been continuous from September 7, 2001 to the present.
