22. Discretion to grant affirmative-relief preliminary injunction. Michigan law authorizes preliminary
    injunctive relief that goes beyond pure status-quo preservation where the underlying facts so warrant.
    See Niedzialek v. Barbers Union, 331 Mich 296, 300 (1951) (preliminary injunction may be granted
    where the relief sought is necessary to preserve the rights of the parties pending final disposition);
    Detroit Fire Fighters Ass'n v. City of Detroit, 482 Mich 18, 34 (2008) (mandatory preliminary injunctions
    are "rare" but available where the moving party makes a particularly strong showing on the four factors).
    The combined showing in Section II elevates this matter above pure status-quo preservation.
23. Narrow scope of supplemental relief. Defendant does not seek pre-judgment adjudication of
    ownership. Defendant seeks only (a) cessation of the specific identified misuse and (b) administrative
    custody, not ownership, pending final judgment. Final ownership remains for the Court to decide on a
    complete record at trial or settlement.

                                           IV. RELIEF REQUESTED
WHEREFORE, in addition to the relief already requested in Part Three of Defendant's Omnibus Motion filed
April 16, 2026, which Defendant continues to request in full, Defendant respectfully requests that this
Honorable Court enter an Order granting the following supplemental relief:
A. Cessation of misuse. Ordering Plaintiff, within seven (7) days of entry of the Order or within such other
period as the Court deems appropriate:
 1. To cease asserting in any forum, sworn or unsworn, that rockenhaus.com is her business domain, her
    workplace, or her property, pending final judgment in this action;
 2. To remove from rockenhaus.com, or to refrain from publishing on rockenhaus.com, any content
    asserting that rockenhaus.com is her business domain, her workplace, or her property; and
 3. To make no further additions, modifications, or deletions to rockenhaus.com pending final judgment,
    except as required to maintain the technical operation of the domain consistent with Part Three of the
    April 16, 2026 Omnibus Motion;
B. Administrative custody pending final judgment. Ordering Plaintiff, within fourteen (14) days of entry of
the Order or within such other period as the Court deems appropriate:
 1. To deliver to Defendant the registrar account credentials for rockenhaus.com (Namecheap, Inc.)
    sufficient to enable Defendant to maintain registration, pay renewal fees from his separate VA disability
    funds, and prevent expiration on September 7, 2026;
 2. To deliver to Defendant the administrative credentials for the email infrastructure associated with
    rockenhaus.com sufficient to restore Defendant's access to his conrad@rockenhaus.com professional
    email account; and
 3. To take no further action that would impair, encumber, transfer, or terminate Defendant's administrative
    custody of rockenhaus.com pending final judgment;
C. Custody is custody, not ownership. Expressly providing that the administrative custody granted under
paragraph B is for the purpose of preserving the asset and stopping the identified misuse pending final
judgment, and is not an adjudication of final ownership, which remains for determination on a complete
record at trial or settlement;
D. Reservation of rights. Expressly providing that Defendant reserves all rights to address the Section II.C
background facts and any other matters not adjudicated by the requested Order through appropriate
procedural channels;
E. Preserving in full all relief previously requested in Part Three of the April 16, 2026 Omnibus Motion;
F. Awarding Defendant his costs incurred in bringing this Supplemental Motion; and
