                              C. Background Facts (Not Operative Predicates)
15. The following additional facts are part of the factual background and are noted here for the Court's
    contextual reference. They are not operative predicates for the supplemental relief requested. The
    supplement's substantive showing rests on Sections II.A and II.B above.
    a. The skyphusion.com domain, registered to Defendant's longstanding online identity "SkyPhusion,"
    used by Defendant pre-marriage in technology and information-security contexts, currently redirects to a
    federal-docket document hosted on rockenhaus.com whose contents post-date and characterize
    Defendant in inflammatory terms.
    b. The current homepage of rockenhaus.com is titled "How the Feds Targeted Adrienne Rockenhaus for
    Speaking Out," a narrative framing that uses the surname-domain to center Plaintiff as protagonist of the
    federal-case narrative.
    c. Public WHOIS history for rockenhaus.com reflects a registrar transfer from Cloudflare, Inc. to
    Namecheap, Inc. occurring while Defendant was incarcerated.
16. Defendant raises the foregoing background facts under MCR 2.116(C) only as context and reserves all
    rights to address them through other procedural channels at the appropriate time. The Court is not asked
    to make findings or grant relief on Section II.C.

                                                III. AUTHORITY

17. The four-factor preliminary-injunction analysis under Pontiac Fire Fighters Union Local 376 v. City of
    Pontiac, 482 Mich 1, 7 (2008), as applied to rockenhaus.com, was set forth in full in Defendant's April 16,
    2026 Omnibus Motion at Part Three and is incorporated herein by reference.
18. Likelihood of success on the merits - unchanged. WHOIS creation date of September 7, 2001
    establishes premarital separate-property status under MCL 552.401 as a matter of objective fact. The
    supplemental facts in Section II.A do not change this analysis; they confirm the inverse, that Plaintiff's
    contrary sworn assertion is unsupported by documentary evidence.
19. Irreparable harm - strengthened. The Section II.B lockout creates ongoing, daily, accumulating harm
    distinct from the lapse-or-transfer harm addressed in the original Part Three. The cessation of misuse
    and the transfer of administrative custody requested below address an active, continuing deprivation that
    money damages cannot redress.
20. Balance of hardships - strengthened. The Section II.A false-swearing materially shifts the balance of
    hardships. Plaintiff's interest in continued exclusive control of rockenhaus.com is no longer a neutral
    status-quo interest. It is an interest that rests on, and is sustained by, a sworn statement contradicted by
    the documentary record. Pontiac Fire Fighters does not require this Court to weigh, in Plaintiff's favor, an
    interest that exists only by reference to a contradicted oath.
