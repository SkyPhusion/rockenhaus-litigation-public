                                            I. INTRODUCTION
1. This Third Chain of Custody Supplement is filed in further support of Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's
   Emergency Motion for Preservation Order and Order Prohibiting Spoliation of Evidence (the "Pending
   Motion"), filed May 13, 2026. This Third Supplement is a continuation of the Chain of Custody
   Supplement filed May 13, 2026 (the "First Supplement") and the Second Chain of Custody Supplement
   filed contemporaneously herewith (the "Second Supplement").
2. This Third Supplement adds to the chain-of-custody record nine additional public-record X-platform
   scrape datasets captured on May 14, 2026 (the "May 14, 2026 Extended Scrape Evidence"). The
   datasets in this Third Supplement fall into three categories: (a) timeline captures of three X accounts in
   the network-adjacent space to the previously-identified amplification network (Joe Prich
   @JustCallMeJoeP, lordofsinatra and BiancaWestfall as a paired capture, and @rosebuttcheeks); (b)
   historical conversation-tree captures of five discrete X conversation threads from the @adezero account
   dating from December 11, 2025 through March 30, 2026; and (c) one negative-result query capture
   documenting an absence of quote-tweets to the May 8, 2026 "Dead Man's Switch" broadcast.
3. This Third Supplement, taken together with the First Supplement and the Second Supplement, provides
   the complete chain-of-custody record for the public-record X scrape datasets supporting the findings
   asserted in Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's Supplemental Brief in Support of Pending Emergency Motion
   for Preservation Order and Order Prohibiting Spoliation of Evidence, filed contemporaneously herewith.
