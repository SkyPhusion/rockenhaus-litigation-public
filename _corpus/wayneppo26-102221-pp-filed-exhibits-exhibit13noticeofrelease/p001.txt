Federal Bureau of Prisons official Notice of Release documenting Respondent Conrad Rockenhaus's

Relevance:
This official BOP document establishes that Respondent was in federal custody, specifically at FCI
Milan SHU for portions of this period, from September 4, 2025 through March 2, 2026. Any allegation of
pre-release contact or harassment by Respondent is constrained by this custodial timeline. The release
date also establishes the temporal window during which Petitioner filed the ex parte PPO (February 20,
2026), served process (March 2, 2026, the release day), and refused Respondent access to the marital
residence, all while Respondent was incarcerated or immediately upon release with no intervening
opportunity to pose any threat.
