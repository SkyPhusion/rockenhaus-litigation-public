                II. DEFENDANT/COUNTER-PLAINTIFF'S POSITION ON THE MOTION
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff does not oppose the substantive relief sought by the Motion to Withdraw. The
Motion asserts a breakdown of the attorney-client relationship and breach of the retainer agreement by
Plaintiff/CounterDefendant. Those assertions concern the internal relationship between Aldrich Legal
Services, PLLC and its client and are not matters on which Defendant/CounterPlaintiff has independent
knowledge or any procedural interest.
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff also takes no position on the assertion in paragraph 5 of the Motion that
"Plaintiff's case will not likely be prejudiced by the withdrawal since the next hearing is not scheduled until
June 4, 2026." The prejudice to Plaintiff/CounterDefendant from the withdrawal of her counsel is a question
for Plaintiff/CounterDefendant and for the Court. Defendant/CounterPlaintiff notes only, and respectfully, that
the case has substantial pending activity beyond the June 4, 2026 Settlement Conference, as cataloged in
the Introduction above and in Section III below.
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff's interest is in an orderly post-withdrawal transition that preserves the integrity of
the existing case record and does not introduce procedural ambiguity into the docket. To that end,
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff respectfully requests the conditions set forth in Section III.
