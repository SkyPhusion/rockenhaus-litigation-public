  DEFENDANT/COUNTER-PLAINTIFF'S RESPONSE TO MOTION TO WITHDRAW AS

NOW COMES Defendant/CounterPlaintiff Conrad Alan Rockenhaus, appearing pro se, and respectfully
submits this Response to the Motion to Withdraw as Counsel filed by Aldrich Legal Services, PLLC and Lisa
R. Baker (P82555) on April 23, 2026 (the "Motion to Withdraw"), as re-noticed for hearing on May 20, 2026
at 9:15 a.m. via Zoom. In support of this Response, Defendant/CounterPlaintiff states as follows:

                                             INTRODUCTION
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff does not oppose the substantive relief sought by the Motion to Withdraw. The
attorney-client relationship between Aldrich Legal Services, PLLC and Plaintiff/CounterDefendant is a matter
between counsel and her client, and Defendant/CounterPlaintiff takes no position on the grounds asserted
by counsel.
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff does, however, respectfully request that any Order granting withdrawal include
specific provisions necessary to ensure an orderly post-withdrawal transition and to protect the integrity of
the existing case record. This case has substantial pending activity. Eight motions by
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff await calendar assignment. Plaintiff/CounterDefendant has a pending Motion to
Set Aside Default of Counterclaim. Defendant/CounterPlaintiff's First Set of Interrogatories and First
Requests for Production of Documents to Plaintiff are pending without complete response. A Domestic
Relations Settlement Conference is scheduled for June 4, 2026. A Motion to Determine Sufficiency, Compel,
and for Discovery Sanctions is in preparation by Defendant/CounterPlaintiff for filing on or about May 19,
2026. The Court's Order should account for these realities so that Plaintiff/CounterDefendant's transition to
pro se status does not disrupt the case progression.

                                      I. PROCEDURAL POSTURE
1. On April 23, 2026, counsel for Plaintiff/CounterDefendant filed the Motion to Withdraw, signed by Lisa R.
   Baker (P82555) on behalf of Aldrich Legal Services, PLLC. The caption of the Motion lists Kenneth R.
   Burger (P73006) as additional counsel of record. The body of the Motion seeks withdrawal of Aldrich
   Legal Services, PLLC and Lisa R. Baker as counsel for Plaintiff/CounterDefendant.
2. On April 29, 2026, this Court held a hearing on Aldrich Legal Services, PLLC's parallel Motion to
   Withdraw in the related Personal Protection Order matter, Wayne County Case No. 26-102221-PP, and
   granted that motion on the record.
