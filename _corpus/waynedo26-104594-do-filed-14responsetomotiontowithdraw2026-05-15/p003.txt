                B. Express Preservation of the Operative Status of Aldrich's Prior Filings
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff respectfully requests that the Order include an express provision that all filings
entered or served by Aldrich Legal Services, PLLC on behalf of Plaintiff/CounterDefendant remain operative,
binding, and attributable to Plaintiff/CounterDefendant following withdrawal. This includes, without limitation:
1. Plaintiff/CounterDefendant's Verified Financial Information Form, filed on or about April 22, 2026;
2. Plaintiff/CounterDefendant's Motion to Set Aside Default of Counterclaim, filed May 13, 2026, and the
   proposed Answer to Counterclaim attached thereto;
3. Plaintiff/CounterDefendant's Answers to Defendant's First Set of Requests for Admissions to Plaintiff,
   served May 13, 2026; and
4. Any other pleading, motion, notice, discovery response, or stipulation filed or served by Aldrich Legal
   Services, PLLC on behalf of Plaintiff/CounterDefendant from the date of Aldrich Legal Services, PLLC's
   appearance through the date of entry of the Order granting withdrawal.
This condition is necessary to prevent Plaintiff/CounterDefendant from later disclaiming, withdrawing, or
repudiating prior filings of record on the basis that she did not personally execute them. It also preserves the
substantive record on which Defendant/CounterPlaintiff's pending motions and discovery requests have
been calibrated.

  C. Express Provision That Pending Discovery Obligations Are Not Stayed, Modified, or Extended
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff respectfully requests that the Order expressly state that the withdrawal of Aldrich
Legal Services, PLLC does not stay, modify, or extend Plaintiff/CounterDefendant's existing discovery
obligations. Defendant/CounterPlaintiff served, by First Class U.S. Mail and electronic transmission pursuant
to MCR 2.107(G), the following discovery requests on April 15, 2026:

1. First Set of Interrogatories to Plaintiff;
2. First Requests for Production of Documents to Plaintiff; and
3. First Set of Requests for Admissions to Plaintiff (responses to which have been served).
Responses to the First Set of Interrogatories and the First Requests for Production of Documents have not
yet been served. The deadlines for those responses have either run or are imminent. This condition is
necessary to ensure that Plaintiff/CounterDefendant cannot use the withdrawal of her counsel as a basis to
seek further extension of, or relief from, discovery deadlines that have already passed.

           D. Production of the Client File to Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant Within Seven Days
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff respectfully requests that the Order direct Aldrich Legal Services, PLLC to deliver
Plaintiff/CounterDefendant's complete client file to Plaintiff/CounterDefendant within seven days of entry of
the Order, with simultaneous written notice to Defendant/CounterPlaintiff confirming the date and manner of
such delivery. This condition is grounded in MRPC 1.16(d), which requires withdrawing counsel to take
reasonably practicable steps to protect a client's interests, including surrender of papers and property to
which    the    client   is   entitled.   Documenting   the   production    with   simultaneous     notice    to
Defendant/CounterPlaintiff prevents any later claim by Plaintiff/CounterDefendant that she is unable to
respond to outstanding discovery, motions, or notices because she does not have access to her case file.
