5. MCR 8.119(I) governs sealed and restricted records and authorizes the Court, on motion, to limit access
    to court records on a showing of good cause. Restriction of access to documents containing
    inadvertently-disclosed protected personal identifying information falls squarely within the rule's purpose.
 6. Federal exposure considerations. Beyond the Michigan Court Rules, the disclosed information
    includes federal benefits-administration identifiers and account routing information for federally-protected
    veterans benefits. Continued public availability of those identifiers presents an ongoing risk of identity
    theft, benefits fraud, and unauthorized access to Defendant's federal benefits accounts.

                       III. PROTECTED INFORMATION REQUIRING REDACTION
              A. Exhibit A to Defendant's Motion for Change of Venue (filed April 15, 2026)

 7. Exhibit A to Defendant's Motion for Change of Venue is a copy of the residential lease for 26695 Ross
    Drive, Redford, Michigan, dated September 1, 2025, between EJK Group LLC as landlord and the
    parties as tenants. The lease is the documentary anchor for the venue argument set forth in the Motion.
 8. The "Received from" header block on the face of the lease contains both parties' Social Security
    numbers. The Social Security numbers are visible on the scanned image and are protected personal
    identifying information under MCR 1.109(D)(9)(a)(ii).
 9. The substantive content of Exhibit A, the lease term September 1, 2025 through August 31, 2026; the
    $1,900 deposit; the parties as joint tenants; the landlord's identity; the property address; the pet
    addendum identifying Toro and Autumn, is not protected and need not be redacted. Only the Social
    Security number block requires redaction.

    B. Exhibit A to Defendant's Brief on Federal Preemption of VA and SSDI Benefits (filed April 15,
                                                     2026)
10. Exhibit A to Defendant's Brief on Federal Preemption is a screenshot of the Department of Veterans
    Affairs Payment History Inquiry (TINQ) for Defendant's VA Disability Compensation account. The TINQ
    screenshot is the documentary anchor for the federal preemption argument and for the financial-
    architecture timeline at issue.
11. The TINQ screenshot displays, on the face of the exhibit, the following protected personal identifying
    information:
a. Defendant's VA File Number, in the field labeled "Fae Number" on the screenshot. VA File Numbers
historically were Social Security numbers and remain government-issued personal identification numbers
within the meaning of MCR 1.109(D)(9)(a)(ii).
b. The routing number for the destination financial account at USAA Federal Savings Bank receiving
Defendant's diverted VA Disability Compensation payments. Routing numbers are financial account
numbers within the meaning of MCR 1.109(D)(9)(a)(v).
c. The full account number for the same destination financial account. Account numbers are financial
account numbers within the meaning of MCR 1.109(D)(9)(a)(v).

12. The substantive content of Exhibit A, the per-payment dates, the per-payment dollar amounts, the source
    institution, the rate step-down between August 2025 and December 2025, the October 1, 2025 returned
    payment marked "Undeliverable," the redirect to the destination account beginning October 9, 2025, is
    not protected and is essential to the federal preemption argument. Only the VA File Number, the routing
    number, and the account number require redaction.
