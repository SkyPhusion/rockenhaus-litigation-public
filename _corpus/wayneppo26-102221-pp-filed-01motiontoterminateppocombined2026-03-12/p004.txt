RESPONDENT’S OMNIBUS MOTION IN LIMINE TO EXCLUDE STALE, FALSE,
PRIVILEGED, AND UNSUBSTANTIATED EVIDENCE
NOW COMES Respondent, Conrad Rockenhaus, in pro per, and moves this Honorable Court to exclude
specific testimony and evidence from the hearing on Respondent's Motion to Terminate the Personal
Protection Order, stating as follows:
I. TO EXCLUDE THE MAY 4, 2023 INCIDENT AS STALE
1. Petitioner relies on an alleged incident from May 4, 2023. Under MRE 402 and 403, an incident
occurring nearly three years ago, followed by years of cohabitation and marriage, is "stale." It does not
demonstrate a "reasonable apprehension of violence" in 2026.
II. TO EXCLUDE THE FALSE "JORJ MICHAEL AUSTIN" ACCUSATION
2. Respondent moves to exclude any testimony regarding an alleged incident involving "Jorj Michael
Austin." This accusation is factually false and based on hearsay. Under MRE 602, Petitioner lacks
personal knowledge of this fabricated event.
III. TO EXCLUDE UNSUBSTANTIATED SEXUAL ASSAULT ALLEGATIONS
