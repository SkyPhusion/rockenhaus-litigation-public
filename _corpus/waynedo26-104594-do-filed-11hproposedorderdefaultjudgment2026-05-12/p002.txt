                D. RETURN OF PERSONAL PROPERTY (COUNTERCLAIM COUNT D)
 6. Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant shall, within seven days of the entry of this Order, return to
    Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff possession of all of Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's personal property,
    including but not limited to: his wallet and all contents (driver's license, VA ID, bank cards); Social
    Security card; birth certificate; DD Form 214; VA Award Letter; Apple iPhone 16e and charger; Apple
    iPad and charger; Apple Mac Mini; backpack with clothing; and all other personal effects.
 7. The cat Autumn, identified in the Counterclaim as marital property jointly adopted during the marriage, is
    reserved for treatment at the Judgment of Divorce stage.

       E. TRANSFER OF PREMARITAL INTERNET DOMAINS (COUNTERCLAIM COUNT E)
 8. Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant shall, within 14 days of the entry of this Order, transfer to
    Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's sole control all of the following premarital internet domains and all
    associated registrar credentials, DNS settings, hosting accounts, and administrative access:

a. rockenhaus.com;
b. skyphusion.com; and
c. greyponyit.com.
 9. Upon failure of Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant to effect the transfer described in Paragraph 8 above within
    the specified period, this Order shall serve as the operative direction to the registrars of record for each
    of the above domains, including but not limited to Network Solutions, GoDaddy, Cloudflare, and any
    successor or replacement registrar, to effect the transfer of the domains, registrar credentials, DNS
    settings, and hosting accounts to Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's sole control without further action by or
    consent from Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant. A certified copy of this Order presented by Defendant/Counter-
    Plaintiff to the registrars of record shall be sufficient authority for the registrars to effect transfer.

         F. CANNABYTES, LLC ACCOUNTING AND RESERVED SHARE DETERMINATION
                              (COUNTERCLAIM COUNT F)
10. Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant shall, within 30 days of the entry of this Order, produce to
    Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff a full accounting of all Cannabytes, LLC revenues, accounts, assets, and
    liabilities from May 20, 2023 through the date of this Order, including but not limited to all bank
    statements, transaction records, customer accounts, vendor accounts, tax filings, and internal financial
    reports.
11. The determination of Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff's equitable share of Cannabytes, LLC is REFERRED to
    a subsequent hearing under MCR 2.603(B)(3)(b)(i) and (ii) following completion of the accounting
    ordered in Paragraph 10 above. The hearing shall be scheduled at a date and time to be set by the
    Court.

  G. RESERVATION OF DISPROPORTIONATE-DIVISION RELIEF (COUNTERCLAIM COUNT G)
12. The disproportionate-division relief sought under MCL 552.401 is RESERVED for treatment at the
    Judgment of Divorce stage. The findings of financial misconduct established by default and reflected in
    the Counterclaim factual averments at Paragraphs 27 through 48 shall carry forward as established facts
    at any subsequent property-division hearing.

                       H. SANCTIONS REFERRED (COUNTERCLAIM COUNT H)
13. The request for sanctions under MCR 1.109(E) is REFERRED to a separate hearing on amount and
    form of sanction. The hearing shall be scheduled at a date and time to be set by the Court.
