 b. Counterclaim Count C (SSDI Component, $12,074.50): reserved for the proper forum (SSA administrative

 on the pending termination motions in Wayne County Case No. 26-102221-PP.
 d. Counterclaim Count K (Transfer or Consolidation): reserved.

                                             K. SETTING ASIDE
16. Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant retains the right to seek to set aside the default underlying this Order within
    21 days of entry, on a showing of good cause and a meritorious defense verified under MCR 1.109(D)
    (3), pursuant to MCR 2.603(D).

                                           L. ENTRY OF ORDER
17. This Order resolves the Motion for Default Judgment on Counterclaim. It is a final order as to the items
    specifically adjudicated above and is interlocutory as to the items reserved or referred for further
    proceedings.
