skyphusion.com is not Conrad Alan Rockenhaus's official engineering site or court record. He states the domain is not under his control and has hosted material related to a federal filing he says a court ordered stricken (Rockenhaus v. Agapiou, W.D. Tex.). His engineering brand sites are skyphusion.net and skyphusion.org. Michigan filings: rockenhaus.net.
