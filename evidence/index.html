Archived exhibits

Archived copies of public social-media posts and correspondence, preserved with capture dates and source identifiers so they can be cited in filings and retraction correspondence.

This page is deliberately not indexed by search engines (noindex, follow). It exists so exhibits remain reachable at a stable URL for litigation use. It is a record of what was posted publicly and when. Descriptions below identify the artifact and its provenance; they are not findings of fact, and no characterisation of any person's beliefs or conduct is asserted here. Allegations belong in filed pleadings, where they are subject to the rules of the court.

Provenance

Each exhibit is a screenshot of a publicly visible post captured on or about the date in its filename. Where a post carries a numeric status identifier, that identifier is given so the original can be located or subpoenaed. Original files are in assets/images/evidence/ and assets/evidence/ in the public repository, whose commit history records when each was added.
